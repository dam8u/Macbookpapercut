**Check 1 — the series.** Nine items, eight semicolons, the advisory last, **before and after**. Store revision 70 left the series at seven items and six semicolons and store revision 71 added two, so the MOO-208 commission's *"seven items"* is counted here in the numbers the series carries. **This cut adds no item and moves none.**

**Check 2 — the markup.** `<u>`/`</u>` **62 / 62 before and after.** Four of the six cures sit inside an open span and would have nested a tag; two sit in the unredlined enactment-clause block. Asserted by applying each cure and re-counting, not by inspection.

**Check 3 — the line diff, which is this sweep's proof and not its delta.** §1 **134,012 → 134,839**; **221 lines before and after**; **three lines changed** — subdivision D 1 **+475**, subsection M 2 **+115**, enactment clause 3 **+237**. [MOO-211](/MOO/issues/MOO-211) R5 replaced the character-delta check with this one, and **this is the first sweep to run it on a revision that writes §1.** A matching delta would also have been produced by two offsetting changes.

**Check 4 — ADVERSE. S1 narrows subsection M's channel and does not close it.** M 2's second sentence deems *"Every determination, **certification**, redetermination, … under this section"* a determination of the Board for the purposes of the subsection, *"whether or not another provision of this section expressly provides for review of it."* **S1's exception is in the failure-to-state limb and the determination limb is untouched**, so a member whose certification omits the advice may bring the identical grievance as a person *"adversely affected by a determination of the Board under this section."* **The act's own words show that this is a route and not a reading:** subdivision D 1 disapplies M 2's second sentence for the member — which it would have no reason to do unless the determination limb were otherwise available — and disapplies it only *"to such a failure"*, which after F3 and S1 no longer includes the advice failure. Cure drafted as **form M-c, +216**, in M 2 after the deeming sentence, resolving 1× on the cut text. **§7 item 137. Not enacted.**

**Check 5 — ADVERSE. S5 does not reach the subdivision G 4 notice.** S5 freezes *"a statement **this subdivision** requires"*, and **subdivision G 4 requires three statements of the effect of provisions of § 51.1-162** in the notice it gives on the member's death: the right under subsection C to waive the allowance, the period that subsection states for the waiver, and the right under subsection B to elect the accumulated contributions. **That notice is the only document a survivor ever receives, and none of the three is frozen.** Cure drafted as **form S5-w, +48 over S5 as enacted** — S5's own sentence generalised to the section and dated by the act's own compound *"that statement, notice, or certification"*, which subsection A already carries. **§7 item 138. Not enacted.**

**Check 6 — nothing enacted outside §1, and the reason §2 needs no conforming edit is not a good one.** §2's plain-English summary, §3 and §4 are untouched. **No conforming edit to §2 is required because §2 does not describe any provision this cut touches**: in 22,876 characters it contains *"designation"* **0 times**, *"beneficiary"* **0**, *"advis-"* **0** and *"failure to state"* **0**, and its single mention of subsection M is the fifth enactment's early-adoption permission. **So the plain-English summary has been silent on the designation-review advisory since store revision 65 created it**, and silent on D 1's compelled descriptions and its failure sentence throughout. That is a gap in the summary and not a defect in the bill, and it is recorded rather than cured because the Editor owns §2's text and nobody has commissioned it. §6.0-73 item 8.
