### The total is +827 and not +807, because two of the approved strings carried mechanical defects

**Both departures are recorded here in full, both are reversible with one word, and neither adds, removes or qualifies a proposition.** The Director approved per-item figures totalling **+807**; the cut totals **+827**.

1. **S3, −4. The approved insert carried a second conjunction into a three-clause series.** It opened *", and that that designation determines…"*. Item 7 is a series of three *"that"* clauses whose single *"and"* belongs to the last — *"…that the member may make or change…, and that nothing in this subdivision requires…"* — so inserting a clause that brings its own *"and"* in front of the last clause yields **"A, B, and C, and D"**. That is the same series defect **substitution 3 of the composite spent four characters to avoid in the outer series**, one revision earlier and in the same sentence. **The inserted clause takes no conjunction: +123, not +127.** The approved form is recovered by adding the word *"and"*.
2. **M3, +24. The approved insert cross-referenced the report by a paraphrase whose only antecedent was the wrong report.** It ended *"…to state a matter in that report,"*. In enactment clause 3 clause (iii) the **only** antecedent for *"that report"* is *"the last report and evaluation required by that subsection"* — the clause names no other report — so the approved words would have continued past the expiry only those provisions requiring a statement in the **last** report, which is not what clause (iii) continues the reporting duties for. **The referent is named in the act's own standing phrase, *"the report required by subsection J"*, which §1 already used 14 times and now uses 15: +119, not +95.** This is the rule against cross-referencing by paraphrase, applied to a paraphrase.

### What the six cures do, and the one thing the Director should read before anything else

**S1 (+115, subsection M 2) is the cure for F3 that the composite could not reach from inside subdivision D 1**, and it is the Director's decision to open subsection M, which the MOO-208 commission had not opened. It excepts the advice failure from M 2's **failure-to-state limb**, in the conformed words the act already uses — so the phrase *"that the Board advises the member to review the member's designation of beneficiary"* now appears **4× in §1**, identical in every place.

**S2 (+118, enactment clause 3 clause (v))** carries the failure-to-state limb of subsection M across the 1 July 2033 expiry, which clause (v) had carried for a determination, for a failure to determine and for a failure to give notice and not for a failure to state. **S3 (+123, item 7)** limits F2's terminus to death before retirement, § 51.1-162 being captioned *"Death before retirement"*, without reversing the Director's F2 decision. **S5 (+310, subdivision D 1)** freezes the descriptions of §§ 51.1-162, 51.1-163, 51.1-165 and 51.1-165.01 that this subdivision compels to the provisions **as they read on the date the certification is given** — the largest of the six and the only one that adds a sentence. **M3 (+119, clause (iii))** continues the provisions requiring the Board to state a matter in the subsection J report, subdivision J 1 naming D 2, D 6, D 7, G 1, G 2, G 3, G 4 and G 5 and not naming D 1. **M4 (+42, item 7)** widens the no-fault clause from the subdivision to the section and to any procedure adopted under subsection M.

**And the finding on the cut text, which is the drafter's and is not enacted: S1 narrows the subsection M channel and does not close it.** Subsection M 2's second sentence deems **a certification** a determination of the Board for the purposes of the subsection, expressly and by name, *"whether or not another provision of this section expressly provides for review of it."* S1 excepts the advice failure from the **failure-to-state** limb of M 2's first sentence and touches the **determination** limb not at all. So a member whose certification omits the advice may still bring the identical grievance as a person *"adversely affected by a determination of the Board under this section."* **The proof that this is a live route and not a reading is in the act's own words, and it is a clause this drafter wrote:** subdivision D 1 disapplies *"the second sentence of subdivision M 2"* for the member — a disapplication that exists for no other reason than that the determination limb is otherwise available — and it disapplies it only *"to such a failure"*, which after F3 and S1 no longer includes the advice failure. **So the one failure the Director has twice decided should not be reviewable is the one failure for which the deeming sentence is left in force.** The better reading is that the specific exception controls; the text does not say so, and **one sentence in M 2 would make it say so**. Drafted as **form M-c, +216, in subsection M 2 after the deeming sentence**, anchored and resolving 1× on the cut text, at §7 item 137 — **and enacted nowhere.** The record should not sit with F3 described as closed.

**And the second finding, which the cross-reference sweep found and which is about the cure the Director approved for the frozen descriptions: S5 does not reach the subdivision G 4 notice.** S5's saving clause is keyed to *"a statement **this subdivision** requires"*, and subdivision D 1 is not the only provision of the section that compels a description of § 51.1-162. **Subdivision G 4 requires the Board to state, in the notice it gives on the member's death, the right under subsection C of § 51.1-162 to waive the allowance, the period that subsection states within which the waiver must be made, and the right under subsection B of that section to elect the accumulated contributions** — three statements of the effect of provisions of § 51.1-162, in **the one document a survivor ever receives**, and none of them is frozen by S5. The gap is the same defect S5 cures, in the worse place. **One sentence cures both**: generalise S5's own sentence to the section and date it by the act's own compound *"that statement, notice, or certification"*, which subsection A already uses. Drafted as **form S5-w, +48 over S5 as enacted**, at §7 item 138 — **and enacted nowhere.**

### One correction to the MOO-211 record, and two things it got right

**C1 — M4's recorded description does not hold.** The MOO-211 kit recorded of M4 that it *"widens the first limb only; the second limb ('or affects any benefit…') stays keyed to the subdivision."* **It does not.** The clause has **one subject** governing both verbs — *"nothing in this subdivision **requires** … or **affects** …"* — so replacing that subject widens both limbs. The consequence is harmless and arguably better, because no provision of § 51.1-142.4 conditions any benefit on the member's having reviewed the designation, so the wider form forecloses an argument nobody should be able to make; **but the description was wrong and the words were approved on it.** The words are enacted as approved and the description is corrected here.

**C2 — subdivision D 1 carries three *"nothing in this subdivision"* clauses and M4 widens one.** The other two are the notice-filing clauses, which concern a duty this subdivision alone creates and are correctly keyed to it. The count moves **3 → 2** for the lower-case formula and the wider *"Nothing in this section"* formula stays at **3**, M4's clause being mid-sentence and lower-cased.

**What MOO-211 got right and this revision confirms: scope135.** The Director confirmed that the exception names **the advice and only the advice**. Item 7 carries one advisory and two propositions of law, so the series carries eight propositions of law and one advisory; widening the exception to all three clauses of item 7 would remove subsection M review from two propositions of law that nobody proposed to remove it from. **No residue, no three-way split, and no (i)–(vii) renumbering.**
