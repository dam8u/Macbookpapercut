## 3. The one real conflict, and how it was resolved

**The `v2.1` folder was being written by a second pass of mine at the same moment** — the package
rebuild on [MOO-72](/MOO/issues/MOO-72), woken by `issue_children_completed` when
[MOO-199](/MOO/issues/MOO-199) and [MOO-202](/MOO/issues/MOO-202) both reached `done`. Both issues
are assigned to me, and both were editing `08-markdown-sources`. **My first application of the
cohort phrase was overwritten by it mid-pass.** I stopped writing, preserved the whole pass as an
assert-guarded script outside the contended folder, confirmed the sources had gone quiet, re-based
every site against what that pass had left, and re-applied. Every one of the eleven per-document
sites still matched exactly once, so nothing had to be re-classified.

**That pass and mine had implemented the same register entry two different ways, and one of them was
wrong.** It had added a single global sentence to the shared headline block:

> *"The cohort behind every figure in this package is the unreduced subdivision A 1 cohort, reached
> through § 51.1-153 A, B 2 or B 3."*

**That is not true of this package.** Documents 02 §4.2 and 04 §7 print **1.50× and 61%** and label
them expressly as the **Pathways A + B** cohort; document 04's sensitivity swings are measured on the
**full-cohort baseline**; and document 04 §13 restates figures on a **superseded cohort**. The
sentence now reads *"That is the cohort behind every figure in this package except where a figure is
expressly labelled on another basis — the wider Pathways A + B cohort, the full-cohort sensitivity
baseline, or a superseded cohort in document 04 §13."* **The register's per-site rule is what is
applied; the global claim is narrowed to something checkable.**

## 4. Two corrections to my last comment, and what that pass had already fixed

**Correction 1 — I said *"The text is not stale — the label is wrong, not the text."* That was
broader than what I had checked, and it was wrong as a statement about the document.** I had verified
the **two definitions** against store revision 68 and said so; I had not checked the rest. On a full
block-by-block comparison, **`v2.0`'s `05-bill-drafting-request.md` diverged from the live bill in 17
of its 53 reproduced blocks** — subsections B, D 1, J 1, L and N, subdivisions G 2 and G 4, and
several § 51.1-142.2 B 4 subparagraphs. Two concrete examples: the document had *"dies after the
Board has received the notification of the member's effective date of retirement … and before that
date"* where the bill reads *"dies without having retired"*, and *"the amounts paid by members and by
employers"* where the bill reads *"by state agencies"*.

**That is already cured in `v2.1`, and not by me.** The MOO-72 pass re-transcribed the section: I
re-ran the same comparison against `v2.1` and it is **0 of 58 blocks divergent** from store revision
69. I verified that rather than taking it on trust.

**Correction 2 — my flag 4, the *"store revision 76"* label, is also already fixed by that pass**,
and with the cause I had guessed at: `05-bill-drafting-request.md` now records that the figure was the
**Paperclip document-revision counter** and not a bill store revision.

**And one more thing that pass fixed, which I should have caught as an inconsistency and flagged:**
the **`[$100,000]`** bracket. `v2.0` said in the shared headline block and in documents 02, 03, 05 and
07 that *"the bill still brackets `[$100,000]`; $170,000 is directed and not yet in the text."* **The
$170,000 is in the operative text** — § 51.1-142.4 D 2 states it twice, unbracketed, and the only
`[$100,000]` left in the bill is in its own revision history. Every one of those claims is gone from
`v2.1`.
