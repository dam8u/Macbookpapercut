## 2. C1 — the Board-delay item. Restated, with three citation repairs, and the subsection J worry withdrawn

### 2.1 Does the mechanism and the cure still read as verified? Yes, and the cure is now larger

All three provisions you named survive at store revision 68:

| Claim | Provision at store revision 68 | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| The notice of retirement may be filed up to 30 days after the Board's written statement | D 1: *"The member shall file the notice of retirement required by § 51.1-153 … no later than 30 days after the date on which the Board gives the member the written statement of the member's earliest qualifying retirement date and final qualifying retirement date"* | **Yes** |
| § 51.1-153 E's 90-day limit is preserved | D 1, twice: *"subject to subsection E of § 51.1-153"* | **Yes, twice** |
| The final qualifying retirement date is extended by the days the statement is late | D 1: *"the member's final qualifying retirement date is extended by the number of days by which the statement or notice is late"* | **Yes** |
| The delay is reported in the subsection J report | D 1: *"the Board shall state every such delay in the report required by subsection J"* | **Yes** — see §2.3 |

**The cure is now in three parts, not two.** Store revision 35 added a final sentence to D 1 requiring the
Board to tell the member, in the statement itself, (i) that the extension operates and how the extended
date rounds, (ii) that she may name the later effective date and that nothing prevents her naming an
earlier one to which she is otherwise entitled, and (iii) that a mailed statement adds three days to
every period running from it. A separate sentence requires the Board to state the 30-day filing period in
that statement too. That is §7 item 27's answer, and **item 27 is closed.**

### 2.2 Three citation repairs — each would send a reader to the wrong provision

1. **"D 1, clause (ii)" → "subdivision D 1".** At store revision 68 the 30-day filing window is a
   free-standing sentence of D 1. **Clause (ii) of D 1 is now something else** — it is part of the
   Board's statement-content duty, about what the Board must *tell* her regarding the prospective
   effective date. Citing the filing window to clause (ii) now points at the wrong text.
2. **Drop the quotation *"the limitation in subsection E of § 51.1-153."*** Those words do not occur at
   store revision 68. D 1 now says *"subject to subsection E of § 51.1-153."* The substance is unchanged
   and the 90-day bound still binds; the **quotation** is not available and must not be printed in quote
   marks.
3. **"D 1's last sentence" → "D 1's penultimate sentence", or better, cite by content.** The extension is
   no longer in the last sentence; store revision 35 put the clause (i)–(iii) statement duty after it.

### 2.3 The subsection J limb — the worry is withdrawn, and nothing changes on this account

**Your premise is a conflation of two different subsections J, and the limb is correct as written.**

The board decision of **11 October 2026** did delete a subsection J. **It was the backfill provision** —
the designator, its three bracketed alternatives, and the closing paragraph carrying the public-safety

**Store revision 10 enacted that deletion and renumbered subsections K through O up one letter.** Row 2 of

reworded."* Row 6 chased the consequence: *"the report required by subsection K"* → *"subsection J."*

So **the Board's annual report has been subsection J since store revision 10** — which is *before* the
narrative revision 21 on which C1 was verified. At store revision 68, *"the report required by subsection
J"* occurs **14 times** in § 51.1-142.4, twice inside D 1, and subsection J is the Board's annual report

**C1's subsection J limb needs no restatement and no withdrawal. Print it as it stands.**

## 5. The cohort phrase — confirmed, with one scope limit that is new since the register

### 5.1 Confirmed for the use the register made of it. Sweep it in

> **the unreduced subdivision A 1 cohort, reached through § 51.1-153 A, B 2 or B 3**

**Still correct, and now evidenced rather than asserted.** MOO-88's published distribution puts 100% of
weight on § 51.1-153 A doors (12.13% + 1.95%), B 2 (85.28%) and B 3 (0.65%), and **nothing on any other
door**. The phrase names exactly the three routes the published figures use, and names them correctly. At
store revision 68 both controlling definitions still require the allowance to be *"calculated in
accordance with subdivision A 1 of § 51.1-155 and … not reduced under subdivision A 2 or A 3 of that
section,"* so *"unreduced subdivision A 1"* is the right description of the door.

**Sweep it into all ten documents verbatim.**

### 5.2 The scope limit — the phrase is short by one route where it describes the admission test

At store revision 68 both *"qualifying retirement"* and *"unreduced retirement eligibility date"* read
*"under § 51.1-153, **or under subsection A or C of § 51.1-155.1**."* That gateway was opened by §7 item
30's answer and drafted in store revision 35 — **after** the register was written. § 51.1-155.1 A and C
are the involuntary-separation and abolished-office routes, and the definitions admit them only where the
member has incurred an involuntary separation or her office has been lawfully abolished. **They carry no
weight in MOO-88's distribution.**

So the phrase is **a complete and correct description of the cohort the fiscal model admits**, and **an
