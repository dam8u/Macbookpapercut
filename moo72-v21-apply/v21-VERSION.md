## 3. What changed, and why

**1. The per-member ceiling is in the bill text.** § 51.1-142.4 D 2 states **$170,000** twice at
store revision 69, where the draft previously bracketed `[$100,000]`. Every document in `v2.0` said
the figure was *"decided and not yet in the text"*; that is now false and is corrected in **ten
places across eight documents**, including the shared lead block and the one-pager. The
*"certified subsidy"* definition the figure is written on is still open and is still carried at R-F.

**2. The bill amends a section `v2.0` did not carry at all.** It now amends **§§ 51.1-142.2 and
51.1-162** and adds § 51.1-142.4. The title, the enacting clause and three subtitles are corrected.
§ 51.1-142.2's subdivision B 4 has also acquired a redetermination right, a no-accrual-after-the-
valuation-date rule and a state agency payment policy that `v2.0`'s transcription did not have, and
the total normal cost rate is now the rate in effect **on the date the member filed the complete
application** rather than on the date the Board accepts the election.

**3. The three must-appear counsel items are in the package. They were in none of the ten
documents in `v2.0`.** That is the one condition this role's own standard calls *not done*.

- **C1 — a Board delay can reach credit the member bought.** Document 07 §3.2 in full, document 03
  §7 as R-I. The mechanism, the three-part cure, and the one residual that survives: the Board's
  notice duty carries no consequence. **Three citations were repaired**, including one quotation —
  *"the limitation in subsection E of § 51.1-153"* — that does not occur in the bill and is not
  printed.
- **C2 — the zero-allowance window.** Document 07 §3.1 in full, document 03 §7 as R-H. Up to **120
  days at zero allowance where the Board meets each of its own deadlines**, the three cases that run
  longer, and the never-notice case for which the bill sets **no outer bound**.
- **C3 — subsection G.** Not a risk entry but a language rule plus one open question, and both are
  in: document 04 §13 item 6 and document 07's *Missing evidence*. The published door distribution
  closes one half of the question — **100%** of expected participants reach an unreduced
  subdivision A 1 door, never to be cited without the **89.26%** no-bill counterfactual beside it —
  and does not reach the other half, which is behavioural, unmodelled and unbounded above.

**Every quotation in C1 and C2 was re-checked against the live § 51.1-142.4 at store revision 69**,
not taken on trust from the revision they were written against. All hold verbatim. The word
*"interest"* occurs **zero times** in § 51.1-142.4, which is what makes the no-interest statement a
reading of the text rather than an inference.

**4. The mandated cohort phrase is in all ten documents, verbatim:** *the unreduced subdivision A 1
cohort, reached through § 51.1-153 A, B 2 or B 3.* The banned form — *"the § 51.1-153 B
normal-retirement cohort"* — remains at zero occurrences.

**5. The workforce exchange: one claim withdrawn, and the figures put behind a marker.** `v2.0` said
the exchange's anchor error ran *against* the proposal and that the real exchange was *worse* than
the documents showed. **That direction is withdrawn** — on a consistent single-design recomputation
the coverage *ratio* is higher than the 71% printed, not lower, because both legs scale with
acceleration. What is genuinely worse is the pension cost per participant, by the **$7,135** `v2.0`
correctly named. **The per-case figures are withheld**: see §4.

## 4. The one figure conflict this version does not resolve

**Two authorised measurements of the workforce exchange disagree, and the disagreement is referred
to the Policy Director rather than settled here.**

| | the recomputation on the recommended design | the authorised chart set |
|---|---:|---:|
| mean acceleration of the authorised cohort | **44.8516 months** | **42.8910 months** |
| net workforce effect, conservative (8%) | −$12,725 | about +$16,000 |
| net workforce effect, central (18%) | +$63,531 | about +$61,000 |
| covers, central | 88.5% | about 85% |
| covers, conservative | **−17.7%** | about **+22%** |

The acceleration is the load-bearing term. The recomputation took its salary and acceleration from
the earlier cohort — its author recorded that the authorised cohort's own statistics were not
available to them — and reasoned that the authorised cohort's mean must be **at or above** 44.85
months, which would make every coverage figure in it a lower bound. The chart set measures that same
mean at **42.8910 months** (`out_moo32_v6_basis.json`), which is **below** it.

**Neither set is adopted.** Document 04 §10 prints both side by side, asserts only what the two
agree on — central coverage of about **85% to 89%** against the **$71,813** anchor, the optimistic
case the only one that covers the cost, and **54% or less** on a present-value-consistent comparison
— and names the quantity in dispute. The chart page built on the second measurement,
`14_v6_11_workforce.png`, is **held** at `04-charts/held-pending-reconciliation/` with its own
`HELD.md`, because a published chart may not print a split the documents decline to print. It is
held, not superseded: nothing is wrong with it on its own basis.

## 6. The caveats that travel, and are not optional

1. **18.4 is an upper bound**, not a measurement of the current text. Clause (viii) of *"eligible
   member"* is in the live draft at store revision 69 and in no committed model run; it is a further
   screen, so it can only refuse more.
2. **The $1.3 million is the cheap end of the retirement window.** The 12-month rung prices every
   member retiring on the last permissible day. One-sided dearer: **+7.5%** on a uniform spread of
   retirement dates, **+16.3%** if every member retires at the earliest.
3. **A refusal is not a trim.** The refused member's whole certified subsidy is **$183,577** central
   against **$9,147** of excess over the ceiling — wrong by a factor of twenty for anyone who reads
   the ceiling as shaving the overage off a funded election.
4. **The cap figures are modelled demand, not observed utilisation**, and at the top of the range the
   appropriation binds and rations.
5. **The contingency inside the cap is still bracketed** at `[20]` percent and is an open figure.
