### §5.38 Store revision 59 — the five enactments and the split, chased in three directions

**Performed by mechanical count over §1 alone, then read individually** — not over the whole document, because §2 and §6 quote the operative text and the item list, now a separate document, quotes it heavily. §1 is **112,886 characters** of the 249,041 this document now holds. **Eleven checks. Two findings, both record-side, both fixed in this revision; no operative defect found.**

**Direction 1 — outward, from the new words to what they cite.**

1. **§ 2.2-4021 read at source on 12 October 2026.** It sits in **Article 3 (Case Decisions)** of Chapter 40 of Title 2.2. Subsection B contains the sentence *"the decision shall be deemed to be in favor of the named party"* after a 90-day period and a further 30 days from a written request; subsection C contains the corresponding sentence running from a hearing officer's recommendation. **Both are described accurately by subdivision M 3 and neither is quoted, so M 3 does not break if the General Assembly amends the words around them.** Its three environmental exceptions are the authority for M 3's form.
2. **Articles 3, 4 and 5 verified as cited.** Article 3 begins at § 2.2-4018, Article 4 is § 2.2-4024 alone, Article 5 begins at § 2.2-4025 — the citation forms the enacted text uses. § 2.2-4001 is the definitions section containing *"case decision"*. § 2.2-4026 A, which the deeming is for, requires a party aggrieved by a case decision.
3. **The new clocks' internal citations.** Subdivision D 3's new sentence cites *"the request required by this subdivision"* — the request is required by D 3's first sentence. Subdivision D 1's new sentence cites *"the confirmation required by subdivision 3"*, which exists, and *"the certification required by this subsection"*, the form subsection C already uses twice for the same instrument. **No citation by paraphrase and no new defined term.**
4. **Subdivision J 1's new trigger** cites *"the report required by this subdivision"* rather than *"that report"*, so the duty does not depend on which report a demonstrative picks up.

**Direction 2 — inward, from the rest of §1 to the sentences that moved.**

Subdivision D 1 **thirteen**, up from twelve. Subdivision D 3 **twelve**, up from eleven. Subdivision J 1 **three**, unchanged. Subdivision M 2 **eight**, up from six. Subsection M now has **three** subdivisions.
6. **Every *"preceding sentence"* antecedent near an insertion point checked by hand.** The one that matters is in subdivision D 1: *"A failure to state in the Board's certification every matter required by the **preceding sentence**…"*, whose antecedent is the long *"shall also state"* sentence. **Both new D 1 sentences are inserted before it, so the antecedent is untouched.** Subdivision D 3 contains no *"preceding sentence"*; nor does subdivision J 1; nor does subsection M.
7. **Sentence-ordinal citations into the amended provisions: zero in §1.** The ordinal citations §1 does contain — *"the first sentence of this definition"*, *"the first sentence of this subdivision"* (D 2 and the G 4 termination-date definition), *"the first sentence of subdivision D 2"*, *"the first sentence of subdivision B 1 of § 51.1-155"*, *"the second sentence of subdivision B 2"* — **none points into subsection C, D 1, D 3, J 1 or M 2.** Counted by regex over §1's 112,886 characters, then read individually.

No enactment clause cites a sentence of any amended provision.
10. **`specified under` still 0** in §1 and in the whole document, holding pass 16's M-2 result.

**Direction 3 — record-side, from the changed text back to everything that describes it.**

11. **Five record statements were falsified by this revision and all five are corrected here, in the same write.** Three said *"D 1's third sentence"* of the sentence beginning *"The Board's certification shall state the matters required by subdivision B 4 c"*, which edit **b** makes D 1's fourth — in §5.37, in §6.0-58, and in an item, where it had said *"third"* of a sentence that was then the fourth and is now the fifth. Two called subsection C's reporting sentence its *"seventh"* when it is its eighth; the sixth and seventh are the two boundary sentences item 111 declined to delete. **In every case the ordinal is replaced by the sentence's opening words rather than renumbered.** The §5.37 bullet recording store revision 58's widening of M 2's class list in *"both occurrences"* gains a pointer, because one of those occurrences is the sentence this revision replaced and the class is carried into the replacement.

**What this sweep did not do.** It was not run over the item list, which is now a separate document and which quotes operative text of every revision from 2 onward; **a verbatim quotation in that document may be of superseded text and should be checked against §1 before it is relied on.** That is a standing property of the list and not a change this revision made, but the split makes it easier to forget. **And the eight substitutions of store revision 58 still have no adversarial pass of their own, as §0.0-58 says.**

### §5.39 Store revision 60 — the thirteen substitutions, chased in both directions

**Result: no conforming amendment is required outside the six provisions amended, and none is made.** Every citation the thirteen substitutions create was opened and read before it was enacted; every provision that cites an amended sentence was read against the amended text.

**Outward — the citations the new words make.**

| New citation | Read where | Holds? |
|---|---|---|
| § 51.1-156 **A** and **B** (item 117(a), *"a ninety-day notification period set forth in subsection A or B"*) | `law.lis.virginia.gov`, 12 October 2026 | Yes. Each permits a member *"in service or within ninety days after termination of service"* to retire for disability. The clause reaches **both** the member still in service, for whom neither subsection sets any period, and the member inside the ninety days — see §6.0-60 item 1. |

**Inward — every provision that reads an amended sentence.**

1. **The two existing *notwithstanding* clauses in G 4** (the rescission sentence and the reversal-settlement sentence) now disapply a bar that no longer catches their cases. **They are left standing and are not deleted**: they are surplusage, not contradiction, and deleting them would re-open the strict-reading argument item 114 exists to close. §6.0-60 item 5.
2. **D 2 and D 3** — the limitation and the contingency amount. Item 114 settles subsidies the bar refused, so an amount that previously stayed charged against the first-sentence limitation of D 2 **permanently** is now settled and remains charged as that subdivision provides for a settled amount. **No amendment to D 2 or D 3 is required**; the reinstatement machinery and its over-cap safeguards (money-committee notice, waiting-list notice, J reporting, no further elections) are reached through G 4's reinstatement limb unchanged.
3. **Subsection N** — unamended, and it now pays on the settlements items 114, 115 and 117 produce, which is the intended effect.
4. **Subdivision G 1** — unamended; item 115's conditioned limb cites it as the settlement provision for the member who **did** retire in a qualifying retirement.
5. **The sentence after item 116's** — *"the amount that this subdivision requires to be included in the next payment of the member's retirement allowance on a reversal or modification"* — reads the amended formula by reference and needs no conforming words.
6. **G 4's notice-of-rescission sentence** and **subsection M 2's deeming sentence** both reach item 103's new determination: the first is unamended because the new determination is not a rescission (it is the trigger for one), and the second because the new sentence says so expressly in the chapter's established form.
7. **J 1's general failure line** — *"every failure of the Board to make a determination, or to give a notice, within a period required by this section, stated separately for each provision"* — already reports a failure to make item 103's determination or to give its notice. **No express report line was drafted for item 103**, which is the minimal footprint. §6.0-60 item 3.
8. **J 2 and J 3** — item 118(b)'s long-stop is confined to a report *"under this subdivision"*, so the Department of Human Resource Management and Department of Accounts report under J 2 and the Director of the Department of Planning and Budget's submission under J 3 are untouched.
9. **Enactment clause 3** — **read clause by clause and no amendment is required.** Clause (i) continues the whole of § 51.1-142.4 for every election accepted before expiry, naming *"every refund, payment, notice, determination, rescission, restoration, and reinstatement required by § 51.1-142.4"* and preserving *"every right of a member under those provisions"* — which carries item 103's determination, item 116's arrears payment and item 120(b)'s refund past 1 July 2033. Clause (iii) continues subsection J *"until the last report and evaluation required by that subsection has been submitted"*, which is now a terminable duty because of item 118(b). **Clause (v)'s saving of subsection M names determinations and notices and not payments**, and that is **not** a gap: clause (v) is the saving for *applications*, and a failure to make a payment can arise only on an accepted election, which clause (i) carries.
10. **Subsection E's second limb** — *"except for the purposes of the definition of 'eligible member' in subsection A, no member shall be treated as having reached his unreduced retirement eligibility date"* — is **not** touched by item 120(a), which amends the **crediting** bar in the same sentence only. **G 3's third sentence**, which disapplies E's second limb on a rescission, therefore reads unchanged.
11. **The third sentence of the definition of *"unreduced retirement eligibility date"*** — the residual date rule — is not displaced by item 119. The new limb is **disjunctive**: the condition is satisfied if the separation or abolition occurred on or before **either** the date the section states (or the third sentence supplies) **or** the member's effective date of retirement. Two rules do not compete for one question.
12. **Subsection F, subsections H, I, K, L and subdivision M 1** — read against all thirteen substitutions; none cites an amended sentence by ordinal or by paraphrase.
13. **Sentence-ordinal citations.** No citation anywhere in §1 points to a sentence of G 4, J 1, M 2, subsection E or the definition by ordinal position, so the two new sentences in G 4 and the one new sentence in J 1 renumber nothing. Checked by searching §1 for *"sentence of"* and for *"preceding sentence"* and reading every occurrence in the amended paragraphs: each *"preceding sentence"* in G 4 still has the same antecedent after the insertion, because item 103's two sentences are inserted **after** a sentence no later sentence points back to, and item 118(b)'s sentence is the **last** of J 1.
