**A BILL to amend and reenact §§ 51.1-142.2 and 51.1-162 of the Code of Virginia and to amend the Code of Virginia by adding a section numbered 51.1-142.4, relating to the Virginia Retirement System; purchase of prior active duty military service credit by state employees; cost; pilot program; **death benefits; designation of beneficiary;** post-retirement employment; reports.**

<u>**D. 1.** The Board shall not accept an election until the Board has, in writing, certified to the member and reserved the certified subsidy for that election under this subsection. The Board shall determine the certified subsidy for an election no later than 30 days after the member's application is completed. The Board shall give the certification required by this subsection no later than 15 days after the confirmation required by subdivision 3 is obtained. The Board's certification shall state the matters required by subdivision B 4 c of § 51.1-142.2, the filing date of the member's complete application, the amount of service credit to be purchased, the certified subsidy, the valuation date, the completion date by which the member must complete payment, the member's anticipated earliest qualifying retirement date and anticipated final qualifying retirement date, the member's unreduced retirement eligibility date determined with the service credit to be purchased as certified, the member's right to cancel the election under subdivision G 5 at any time before completing payment, and the member's obligations under subsections E, F, and G. The Board shall not accept an election until the member has filed with the Board a written acknowledgment of the member's obligations under subsections E, F, and G. The Board's certification shall also state that, where a member whose election the Board has accepted dies without having retired, the benefit payable on the member's death is determined under subdivision G 4 and under § 51.1-162 as on the death of a member in service and not under the provisions of subdivision C 1 of § 51.1-165 or of subsection F of § 51.1-165.01 that require benefits to be paid in accordance with § 51.1-163; that where the member dies while in service and subdivision C 2 of § 51.1-165 otherwise applies, that subdivision provides for the payment of benefits in accordance with § 51.1-163, except that where the contingent annuitant or annuitants under the option elected are the same person or persons who would receive a monthly benefit under subsection B of § 51.1-162 and that monthly benefit would be greater, that subdivision provides for the payment of benefits in accordance with that subsection, and that the Board shall pay the benefit that subdivision provides or the benefit determined under subdivision G 4, whichever is greater; that no option elected under § 51.1-165.01, and no option elected under subdivision A 2 or A 3 of § 51.1-165, limits that benefit, but that the straight life option under subdivision A 1 of § 51.1-165 is an election to receive an increased retirement allowance in lieu of any death benefits and, where that election is not rendered null and void by subdivision C 1 of § 51.1-165, may leave no benefit payable on the member's death; that, to the extent required by § 401(a)(37) of the Internal Revenue Code, as amended or renumbered, and the regulations thereunder applicable to governmental plans, subsection B of § 51.1-162 provides a retirement allowance on the death of a member in service only where no benefit is payable under subsection C of that section and only to a surviving spouse, minor child, or parent whom the member has designated under subsection A of that section or who is entitled in the order of precedence that subsection B of that section states where the member has made no designation or the person designated has predeceased the member and no other designation has been made;~~ that the member is advised to review the member's designation of beneficiary under subsection A of § 51.1-162 before completing payment;~~ that subsection C of § 51.1-162 provides a retirement allowance, where the member dies in service from a cause compensable under the Virginia Workers' Compensation Act (§ 65.2-100 et seq.), to the member's surviving spouse, minor children, or parents as that subsection provides;~~ and~~ that where no benefit is payable under subsection B of § 51.1-162, subsection A of that section provides for the payment of the member's accumulated contributions, reduced by the amount of any retirement allowance previously received by the member, to the person the member has designated or, where the member has made no designation or the person designated has predeceased the member and no other designation has been made, to a surviving relative in the order of precedence that section states**; and that the Board advises the member to review the member's designation of beneficiary under subsection A of § 51.1-162, and the effect of that designation under subsection B of that section, before completing payment, that the member may make or change that designation under subsection A of that section at any time before the member's death, and that nothing in this subdivision requires the member to review the designation or affects any benefit by reason of the member's not having reviewed it**. A failure to state in the Board's certification every matter this subdivision requires to be stated in it**, other than a failure to state that the Board advises the member to review the member's designation of beneficiary,** is a failure of the Board to state in a certification a matter this section requires to be stated in it for the purposes of subsection M; a person adversely affected by it may seek review under subsection M no later than 30 days after the date on which the certification is given to the member or, where that person is not the member, no later than 30 days after the date on which the notice required by subdivision G 4 is given to that person; the second sentence of subdivision M 2 does not apply to such a failure where the person adversely affected is the member; and the Board shall state every such failure**, and every failure to state that the Board advises the member to review the member's designation of beneficiary,** in the report required by subsection J. The member shall file the notice of retirement required by § 51.1-153, stating as the effective date of retirement a date on which the member will retire in a qualifying retirement, no later than 30 days after the date on which the Board gives the member the written statement of the member's earliest qualifying retirement date and final qualifying retirement date first required by the definition of "final qualifying retirement date" in subsection A. Nothing in this subdivision requires the member to file that notice any period before the effective date of retirement stated in it. A notice filed within the period allowed by this subdivision satisfies § 51.1-153 notwithstanding that it is filed after the effective date of retirement stated in it; and where such a notice is filed after that effective date, the member's retirement shall take effect on that date and the retirement allowance shall be payable retroactively to that date, subject to subsection E of § 51.1-153 and provided that the effective date stated is on or after the day following the member's last day of service. The Board shall state the period allowed by this subdivision in the written statement required by that definition. Where the Board gives that written statement, any notice of a later approved date required by that definition, or any notice of a date required by that definition upon the Board's action upon a request, later than the time required by that definition, the member's final qualifying retirement date is extended by the number of days by which the statement or notice is late, the member may state an effective date of retirement that is no earlier than the first day of the month following the month in which the Board gives the statement or notice, and the Board shall state every such delay in the report required by subsection J. The Board shall state, in the written statement required by that definition and in every notice of a later approved date required by that definition, (i) that where the Board gives the statement or notice later than the time required by that definition the member's final qualifying retirement date is extended by the number of days by which the statement or notice is late, that the date so extended is subject to the limits stated in that definition, and that where the date so extended is not the first day of a month it is the first day of the next month; (ii) that the member may state as the effective date of retirement a date no earlier than the first day of the month following the month in which the Board gives the statement or notice, and that nothing in this subdivision prevents the member from stating an earlier effective date of retirement to which the member is otherwise entitled, subject to subsection E of § 51.1-153; and (iii) that where the Board mails the statement or notice, every period that runs from the giving of it is extended by three days.</u>
