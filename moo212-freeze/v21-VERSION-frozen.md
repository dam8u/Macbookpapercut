## 3. The freeze gate, and what each of its five items produced

**The Policy Director's MOO-206 decision document set five conditions, and `v2.1` could not freeze
until all five were met.** They are recorded here because the freeze record is the only place a later
reader can check them.

**1. The bill-dependent surface re-verified at one live revision, and every dateline set to it.**
A dateline states the revision the text was **checked at**; it is not a label, and nobody advances
one by editing the number. Every quotation of the bill in the ten documents was counted against the
live § 51.1-142.4 at store revision 71, inside the section rather than over the whole bill: **nine
quotations, all holding, each at the count printed beside it.** Eleven claims the package states as
counts rather than as quotations were counted too, and all eleven hold — $170,000 twice in D 2, the
`[$100,000]` bracket at zero, clause (viii) of *"eligible member"* present, § 51.1-155 cited once in
subdivision B 4 a of § 51.1-142.2 and in four places in § 51.1-142.4, *"interest"* at zero in
§ 51.1-142.4, the contingency still bracketed at `[20]`, the cap at $3,000,000, and the amended
sections and the title line as reproduced. **Then, and only then, 29 datelines were set to store
revision 71.** The package carries no other revision as a dateline. The sweep is scripted with the
verification as its gate: it refuses to move a number if the surface does not verify.

**2. C1 re-verified at that revision, with the distinction it was not drawing made express.**
Revisions 70 and 71 both changed **subdivision D 1**, which is the subdivision C1 is about, so C1
was re-checked rather than carried forward. Its mechanism, its three-part cure and its residual all
survive, and **its printed counts are unchanged** — *"the report required by subsection J"* still
**14** times in § 51.1-142.4, twice inside D 1, and *"for the purposes of subsection M"* still
**6**. What was missing was a distinction a reader would have challenged: **D 1's consequence
sentence runs on the Board's *certification*, and C1's residual runs on the Board's written
*statement* of qualifying retirement dates.** That is now stated in terms, in document 07 §3.2 and
in the memo-length form at document 03 §7 R-I, together with what each revision changed — revision
70's four new certification matters, the carve-out for the beneficiary-designation advisory and the
extended subsection J limb, and revision 71's move of the 15-day certification clock, its narrowing
of the § 51.1-165.01 limb and the words *"under this chapter or the abolished system"*. **None of it
reaches the written statement, so the residual stands.** C1 was re-verified, so it is in and not
withdrawn.

**3. Document 05's reproduced bill text re-transcribed, and the block comparison re-run to zero.**
*"0 of 58 blocks divergent"* was true at revision 69 and was not true after. The comparison was
rebuilt as a script rather than a reading, so it finds divergence instead of relying on somebody to
notice it: **2 blocks divergent at revision 70, 7 at revision 71.** Each was matched to exactly one
block of the live bill, the match was required to be unambiguous, and the replacement was **lifted
out of the live document rather than retyped**. The blocks: the title line, subdivision D 1,
subdivision G 4's death branch, subdivisions M 2 and N, the § 51.1-162 C conforming amendment, and
enactment clause 3's new clause (vi). The editorial note's garbled §0 reference was re-transcribed
with them. **The comparison now reads 0 of 98 blocks divergent** — 98 rather than 58 because the
rebuilt comparison splits the reproduced span finer; the block count is a property of the
comparison, and the result is the same claim.

**4. The nine liftable rows cured, and the one unclassified site reported.** The back-reference rule
stands: the 14-word cohort phrase in full at the first class-1 site in each document, at any class-1
site beside a differently-labelled cohort and at any class-1 site carrying a headline figure, a
back-reference elsewhere. **The guard is that a back-reference may not stand in anything that can be
lifted out alone** — a table row or cell, a caption, a chart or axis label, a slide title, an
executive-summary line. Where the full phrase does not fit, the minimum is the identifier, *"the
unreduced subdivision A 1 cohort"*, and never a bare *"the cohort"*. **Eleven occurrences in nine
table rows across six documents are cured** — `00-cover-and-contents.md` (the withheld-figures row,
the ceiling row), `02-policy-brief.md` (the ceiling row), `03-decision-memo.md` (the closed-item row,
twice), `04-fiscal-analysis.md` (the $80,000 row, the Plan 2 row), `05-bill-drafting-request.md`
(element 9, the closed-item row), `07-risk-register.md` (R-F, twice).

**Two sites were deliberately left alone and are asserted to survive the sweep.** The shared headline
block's *"4.74% of that cohort"* back-references the full phrase eight lines above in the same
quote-block, which never travels in halves. And `04-fiscal-analysis.md` §13's *"$1,641,824 on that
cohort"* back-references the **superseded** cohort named in its own row; the authorised cohort's name
there would mislabel the figure.

**And one site is reported rather than classified, because the classification is not the Editor's.**
`07-risk-register.md`'s **R1 / F1 / F2** row reads *"the cohort was rebuilt monthly from membership
history with the pathways made mutually exclusive."* It **is** a table row, so on the guard it is
liftable; but it describes how the model's cohort was constructed rather than naming the cohort
behind a figure, so it may be no class at all. It is unchanged, and it is the Policy Director's to
classify. A sweep of every cohort back-reference in the package found no other liftable site: the
remaining occurrences are prose, list items, or the shared headline quote-block.

**5. The lead-in corrected at all five sites, and the note carried once per document.** A lead-in
stays: three of the five sites sit beside the pre-existing words *"One pathway only"*, and without
one the row reads as *only* those routes — the expressio unius argument reserved at item 47. But
*"which the bill defines as"* handed our reading to the bill, which cites **§ 51.1-153 whole** and
never writes *"A, B 2 or B 3."* **Four words changed and nothing else:** the row now reads *"…which
is, under the bill, reached through § 51.1-153 A, B 2 or B 3, or, on an involuntary separation or a
lawfully abolished office, through subsection A or C of § 51.1-155.1."* No *"only"* is bound to the
route list, and neither § 51.1-155.1 B nor § 51.1-155.2 A is named anywhere in the package. **Five
sites changed** — documents 02, 03, 05 and 06, the last of which has two. And the note is carried
**once per document at the first extended-form site, inside the block that carries the route list**
rather than as a separate paragraph under it, so that a lifted table row takes the note with it:

> *The bill's definitions cite § 51.1-153 as a whole, and subsection A or C of § 51.1-155.1. The
> subdivisions of § 51.1-153 named here are the routes within that section that reach an allowance
> calculated under subdivision A 1 of § 51.1-155 and not reduced under subdivision A 2 or A 3.*

## 4. What changed from `v2.0`, and why

**1. The per-member ceiling is in the bill text.** § 51.1-142.4 D 2 states **$170,000** twice at
store revision 71, where the draft previously bracketed `[$100,000]`. Every document in `v2.0` said
the figure was *"decided and not yet in the text"*; that is now false and is corrected in **ten
places across eight documents**, including the shared lead block and the one-pager. The
*"certified subsidy"* definition the figure is written on is still open and is still carried at R-F.

**2. The bill amends a section `v2.0` did not carry at all.** It now amends **§§ 51.1-142.2 and
51.1-162** and adds § 51.1-142.4. The title, the enacting clause and three subtitles are corrected.
§ 51.1-142.2's subdivision B 4 has also acquired a redetermination right, a no-accrual-after-the-
valuation-date rule and a state agency payment policy that `v2.0`'s transcription did not have, and
the total normal cost rate is now the rate in effect **on the date the member filed the complete
application** rather than on the date the Board accepts the election.

**3. The counsel items the red team marked must-appear are in the package. They were in none of the
ten documents in `v2.0`.** That is the one condition this role's own standard calls *not done*.

- **C1 — a Board delay can reach credit the member bought.** Document 07 §3.2 in full, document 03
  §7 as R-I. The mechanism, the three-part cure, and the one residual that survives — with the
  statement/certification distinction now express, and with what revisions 70 and 71 did to
  subdivision D 1 recorded in the entry. **Three citations were repaired**, including one quotation
  — *"the limitation in subsection E of § 51.1-153"* — that does not occur in the bill and is not
  printed.
- **C2 — the zero-allowance window.** Document 07 §3.1 in full, document 03 §7 as R-H. Up to **120
  days at zero allowance where the Board meets each of its own deadlines**, the three cases that run
  longer, and the never-notice case for which the bill sets **no outer bound**. **Subdivision G 2 and
  subsection F, the two provisions C2 states, are word-for-word unchanged across revisions 69, 70
  and 71.**
- **C3 — subsection G.** Not a risk entry but a language rule plus one open question, and both are
  in: document 04 §13 item 6 and document 07's *Missing evidence*. The published door distribution
  closes one half of the question — **100%** of expected participants reach an unreduced
  subdivision A 1 door, never to be cited without the **89.26%** no-bill counterfactual beside it —
  and does not reach the other half, which is behavioural, unmodelled and unbounded above.

**The ten remaining C-series items — C4, C5, C6, and C12 to C18 — are in no artifact, and they did
not hold this freeze.** The published register is the R-series, which is complete in itself; adding
R-H and R-I is not a claim that the C-series is exhausted. They are commissioned as a separate
drafting pass, with the register reconciliation for C3, C19, C20, C21, C22 and C23 to be done first.

**4. The mandated cohort phrase is in all ten documents, verbatim:** *the unreduced subdivision A 1
cohort, reached through § 51.1-153 A, B 2 or B 3.* The banned form — *"the § 51.1-153 B
normal-retirement cohort"* — remains at zero occurrences. The global sentence that once said this was
the cohort behind *every* figure in the package is **narrowed**, because it is not true of the
Pathways A + B pair, the full-cohort sensitivity baseline or the superseded cohort in document 04
§13.

**5. The workforce exchange: one claim withdrawn, and the figures put behind a marker.** `v2.0` said
the exchange's anchor error ran *against* the proposal and that the real exchange was *worse* than
the documents showed. **That direction is withdrawn** — on a consistent single-design recomputation
the coverage *ratio* is higher than the 71% printed, not lower, because both legs scale with
acceleration. What is genuinely worse is the pension cost per participant, by the **$7,135** `v2.0`
correctly named. **The per-case figures are withheld**: see §6.

## 5. The one figure conflict this version does not resolve

**Two authorised measurements of the workforce exchange disagree, and the disagreement is referred
to the Policy Director rather than settled here.**

| | the recomputation on the recommended design | the authorised chart set |
|---|---:|---:|
| mean acceleration of the authorised cohort | **44.8516 months** | **42.8910 months** |
| net workforce effect, conservative (8%) | −$12,725 | about +$16,000 |
| net workforce effect, central (18%) | +$63,531 | about +$61,000 |
| covers, central | 88.5% | about 85% |
| covers, conservative | **−17.7%** | about **+22%** |

The acceleration is the load-bearing term. The recomputation took its salary and acceleration from
the earlier cohort — its author recorded that the authorised cohort's own statistics were not
available to them — and reasoned that the authorised cohort's mean must be **at or above** 44.85
months, which would make every coverage figure in it a lower bound. The chart set measures that same
mean at **42.8910 months** (`out_moo32_v6_basis.json`), which is **below** it.

**Neither set is adopted.** Document 04 §10 prints both side by side, asserts only what the two
agree on — central coverage of about **85% to 89%** against the **$71,813** anchor, the optimistic
case the only one that covers the cost, and **54% or less** on a present-value-consistent comparison
— and names the quantity in dispute. The chart page built on the second measurement,
`14_v6_11_workforce.png`, is **held** at `04-charts/held-pending-reconciliation/` with its own
`HELD.md`, because a published chart may not print a split the documents decline to print. It is
held, not superseded: nothing is wrong with it on its own basis.

## 7. The caveats that travel, and are not optional

1. **18.4 is an upper bound**, not a measurement of the current text. Clause (viii) of *"eligible
   member"* is in the live draft at store revision 71 and in no committed model run; it is a further
   screen, so it can only refuse more.
2. **The $1.3 million is the cheap end of the retirement window.** The 12-month rung prices every
   member retiring on the last permissible day. One-sided dearer: **+7.5%** on a uniform spread of
   retirement dates, **+16.3%** if every member retires at the earliest.
3. **A refusal is not a trim.** The refused member's whole certified subsidy is **$183,577** central
   against **$9,147** of excess over the ceiling — wrong by a factor of twenty for anyone who reads
   the ceiling as shaving the overage off a funded election.
4. **The cap figures are modelled demand, not observed utilisation**, and at the top of the range the
   appropriation binds and rations.
5. **The contingency inside the cap is still bracketed** at `[20]` percent and is an open figure.

## 8. How this was verified

Every check below is a script kept with the build record at `moo212-freeze/` in the project
repository, run against the files rather than against notes, exiting non-zero on a miss.

- **`verify_surface.py`** — every quotation of the bill in the ten documents, counted inside
  § 51.1-142.4 at the live revision, plus the eleven counted claims and the three strings the
  package states do **not** occur in the bill. **Nine quotations hold; the three withdrawn strings
  are at zero; eleven of eleven counted claims pass.** Ten further quoted strings are listed with
  the reason each is not the bill's words — another section of the Code, the opposition's framing, a
  withdrawn claim of ours — so a non-bill quotation cannot pass as a missing one.
- **`blocks.py`** — document 05 §4's reproduction, block by block, against the live text.
  **0 of 98 blocks divergent.**
- **`retranscribe.py`** — re-transcription driven by that comparison rather than by reading, with
  every replacement lifted out of the live document and every match required to be unambiguous.
- **`set_datelines.py`** — the dateline sweep, gated on the two above passing in the same run
  against the same live file. **29 datelines set to store revision 71**, with the historical
  references to revisions 69 and 70 protected by name and asserted to survive.
- **`sweep_liftable.py`** — the eleven liftable back-references, each anchored to a line that occurs
  once, with the two protected sites asserted intact afterwards.
- **`fix_leadin.py`** — the five lead-ins and the note, asserting afterwards that no document retains
  the old lead-in, that no *"only"* is bound to the route list, and that § 51.1-155.1 B and
  § 51.1-155.2 A are named nowhere.
- **`install_renders.py`** — the thirty artifacts built from one source state in one pass, the
  sources checksum-matched against what the renders were built from, every render newer than every
  source, the one-pager counted from the PDF page tree, and the zip rebuilt and compared entry by
  entry. **72 entries, zero mismatches.**
- **The MOO-72 release checks, `verify_v21.py`, re-run unchanged: 22 of 22 pass**, including the
  recommendation sentence at one SHA-256 across all ten, the banned cohort phrase at zero, the
  28-token retired set carrying no live claim, and all four travelling caveats in all ten documents.

`MANIFEST-v2.1.sha256` records the SHA-256 of every published artifact as this freeze left it, so
