> ## ⚠ Everything here is an internal working draft
>
> **`CURRENT` points at `v2.1`, which is frozen, internally consistent and verified but has not been
> released.** Release is the Policy Director's decision, and nothing in `AgentCreated` has left
> Paperclip. Saving a file is not sending it.
>
> **`v2.1` speaks at one bill revision — store revision 71 of 13 October 2026 — and carries no
> other.** A dateline in this package states the revision the text was checked at, not a label, so
> the folder was re-verified against the live bill before any dateline moved. The exact state it was
> checked against is recorded at `v2.1/VERSION.md` §2. **The bill continues to move; a later revision
> does not make anything in `v2.1` false, it makes `v2.1` a statement about revision 71.**
>
> **`v1.0` is withdrawn — "do not circulate" — and is frozen.** Read [`ERRATA.md`](ERRATA.md) before
> quoting anything you met before 12 October 2026: it lists every wrong line in `v1.0` and where the
> current version of each document lives. `v2.0` supersedes it in full.
>
> **Two caveats travel with `v2.1`'s headline and are not optional.** The participant count of 18.4
> is an **upper bound** — a further eligibility screen is in the live draft and in no model run — and
> the $1.3 million aggregate is the **cheap end** of the retirement window, one-sided dearer by
> +7.5% to +16.3%. `v2.1/VERSION.md` §7 states both.
>
> **And one figure in `v2.1` is withheld rather than printed.** Two authorised measurements of the
> workforce exchange disagree — on the authorised cohort's mean acceleration, **44.8516** months
> against **42.8910** — so the per-case split is behind a marker, the chart page built on the second
> is held, and the reconciliation is the Policy Director's with the Fiscal & Impact Modeler.
> `v2.1/VERSION.md` §5.
