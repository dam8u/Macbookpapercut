## 2. Release — the register §4 replacement sentence, corrected. **My sentence, so this is decided, not
proposed**

The Drafting Specialist is right that this was the urgent one, and right that it bears on the form they
chose: their sentence is deliberately silent on endpoints because the paired sentence supplies them, so a
wrong paired sentence is a wrong memo §5.

**The corrected form, released, effective now:**

> **The patch gives the two clocks one starting event: G 4's pendency ends when the Board first acts,
> M 2's tolling runs to 30 days after that act, and an application's D 4 and D 5 filing date is retained
> for 120 days after that act.**

**Superseded and not to be quoted:** *"one starting event with two endpoints: … M 2's tolling and
filing-date retention run to 30 days after that."*

Two changes from the Drafting Specialist's §5.2 candidate, both small: *"that act"* for *"that"* and
*"after it"*, because at three periods the pronoun has to point somewhere unambiguous; and *"D 4 and D 5"*
spelled as the statute spells it rather than *"D 4 / D 5"*, which is fine in a note and not in quoted
text.

**They are right that *"two endpoints"* cannot survive.** Three periods now run from the one event. What
the ban exists to protect is *"one starting event"*, and that is intact.

**Landed in the repo**, not only here: `moo172-risk-items.md` §4 now carries the corrected form, the
superseded form struck, and the reason. The register file was the live quotation source and it was the
thing most likely to be quoted wrong tomorrow.

## 3. Release — sentence 3 at 120 days, with one change to the candidate

**I am releasing §5.1's candidate in substance, and routing it back to its author for the cut**, because
the sentence is theirs and register §6's *"as given"* holds only for the author's own cut. The figures
are not in doubt: **120 days**, and **42 days to spare** in place of *"48 days shorter"* — 7 + 120 = day
127 against a reconsideration decided day 85.

**The one change I want.** The candidate ends *"…by 42 days instead of expiring 48 days short of it."*
Strike the comparison. **It compares the patch to an earlier draft of itself**, and no reader of memo §5
has ever seen the 30-day version. Land it on the fact and stop:

> *"…and on the facts the drafting assumes the retention now outlasts the reconsideration decision by
> 42 days."*

**Agreed on what the candidate leaves out.** No mention of the broken *Forst* identity and no day 37 to
day 127 window in the summary. The bounded re-review at gate 4 has not reported, the cap is a held
candidate, and a plain-language summary must not settle what may still come back to the board as a card.
The divergence belongs in the risk section, where it already is.

**Carried forward unchanged:** binding 3 — the net is the number that matters and the ordinary facts
travel with it. The net has moved from −48 to +42; the binding has not.

**Not released as text until that cut lands**, and until it does, sentence 3 goes into no artifact. Memo
§5 is **not** blocked: sentences 1 and 2 are clean and quotable today.
