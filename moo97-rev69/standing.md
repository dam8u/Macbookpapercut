# Standing drafting directions — new § 51.1-142.4

**Purpose.** This document exists because one sentence of subdivision G 4 has now been cut four times and three of those cuts drew a **fatal** finding from the next bounded counsel pass. Each cure was reasonable on its own facts and each reversed the one before it. A direction that lives only in a §6 per-revision record is a direction the next revision does not read. **Everything in Part A is binding on every future revision of `bill-text-s8` and may be reversed only by the Policy Director in terms.**

Internal working draft throughout. Nothing here is a Division of Legislative Services bill, nothing has been reviewed by DLS, and no part of it is legally sufficient for introduction.

## Part A — Directions from the Policy Director (binding)

### A1. Subdivision G 4's death-benefit condition stays a condition on the member's retirement status

**Direction, 12 October 2026, out of [MOO-188](/MOO/issues/MOO-188), on this issue's thread.** The condition on subdivision G 4's death-benefit sentence

> *"where clause (i) of this subdivision applies and the member has not retired before the date of death"*

**stays a condition on the member's retirement status and never returns to a filing, notification, acceptance or Board-receipt event.** The Director verified both legs on the live body: *"dies after the Board has received the notification"* occurs **0** times in the document, and the enacted condition occurs **1** time in the document and **1** time in §1.

**Why the direction exists — the sentence has two opposite failure modes, and each has already been fatal once.**

| Cut | Form of the condition | Finding against it |
|---|---|---|
| Store revision 45 | deeming deleted, nothing put in its place | [MOO-126](/MOO/issues/MOO-126) **F1 fatal** — § 51.1-165 C 1 took command of the filed-notice death |
| Store revision 58 | governed only by clause (i) — *"dies"* | [MOO-161](/MOO/issues/MOO-161) **F1 fatal** — reached a member who had **retired** and died years later, forfeiting her own unrecovered contributions |
| Store revision 61 | confined to the Board-receipt-to-effective-date **window** | [MOO-169](/MOO/issues/MOO-169) **F1 fatal** — missed the member whose position is abolished, whom subdivision D 1 expressly tells she need not file early |
| Store revision 62 | **the enacted form above** | accepted by the Director; under bounded pass [MOO-189](/MOO/issues/MOO-189) |

**Too wide reaches the retiree. Too narrow by any filing event excludes the member who never filed.** The enacted condition is the negative of the carve-out clause (i) already states in its own second sentence, so it carries the retiree carve-out without importing an event the member may never perform.

**Two consequences for a future reviewer's fix direction.** A fix direction that proposes *"before the effective date of any retirement of the member"* is **weak for the core of the class** — a member who never retired has no effective date for her death to precede; this is the correction this role made to MOO-169's own F1 fix direction and the Director has adopted the reasoning. A fix direction that proposes any filing, notification, acceptance or Board-receipt event is **foreclosed by A1** and must be answered by saying so, not by drafting it.

### A5. Route (b) on §7 item 130 — M 2's uncapped tolling — is HELD, not declined, and is not a conforming amendment

**Direction, 12 October 2026, out of [MOO-141](/MOO/issues/MOO-141), on this issue's thread.**

> **MOO-164 takes the question first, with all three limbs in front of it. Route (b) is held here and reconsidered on what MOO-164 decides — it may be made redundant by (c).**

**Three things follow, and all three are binding.**

1. **Route (b) is not to be drafted** — not on [MOO-97](/MOO/issues/MOO-97), not on [MOO-164](/MOO/issues/MOO-164), and not in any store revision — until the Director releases the hold. Route (c) was already not to be drafted, because it decides what it is to *"act upon"* a request and that belongs to MOO-164.
2. **Route (b) must never be written up as a conforming amendment.** It carries subdivision G 4's words — *"a failure of the Board to act upon such a request within the period allowed to it for acting upon the request shall not extend that aggregate"* — into subdivision M 2, **where the incidence reverses.** G 4's non-running protects *"a period within which the Board is required to act or a period within which a determination is required to take effect"*, so denying the extension makes the Board bear its own delay. M 2's tolling protects *"the member's final qualifying retirement date and … any period within which the member or applicant must act"*, so the identical words make **the member** bear it. Verified on the live text of both. **It is a policy choice wearing a conforming amendment's clothes.**
3. **Held is not declined.** The Director said in terms that it is still possibly the right choice.

**And the triage's premise is now known to be under-inclusive.** There are **four** endpoints keyed to the Board acting upon a request, not three: M 2's tolling terminus, M 2's subdivision D 4 / D 5 filing-date retention, M 2's 15-day duty to act upon the application, **and a fourth in subsection A's definition of *"final qualifying retirement date"*** — the Board's 15-day duty to notify the member of that date — which **route (b) cannot reach because it is not in M 2, and route (c) cannot reach as described because store revision 63's deemed denial is confined to a request *for reconsideration* and a request under that definition has no deemed denial at all.** Counted and quoted at [`moo97-m2-act-endpoint-inventory`](/MOO/issues/MOO-97#document-moo97-m2-act-endpoint-inventory).

### A6 — The act amends **two** sections and adds one, and § 51.1-162 is reproduced in full in §1

**New in store revision 67, and binding.** The title line reads *"to amend and reenact §§ 51.1-142.2 **and 51.1-162** of the Code of Virginia and to amend the Code of Virginia by adding a section numbered 51.1-142.4"*, the first enactment clause follows it, and **§ 51.1-162 appears in §1 in Code order after § 51.1-142.4**, reproduced in full from the official LIS text read at source on 12 October 2026, with exactly **one** underlined clause in the third paragraph of subsection C: *", except as subdivision G 4 of § 51.1-142.4 provides,"*.

## Part B — Drafting-practice rules this issue established the hard way

These are this role's rules, not the Director's directions. Each is recorded where it was learned.

- **B1. One formulation per concept, and the act's own vocabulary wins.** *"The person entitled to any benefit payable on the member's death"* now appears in three places and is the only phrase for that class; *"this chapter"*, not *"the chapter"*; the exclusion in subsection L is in the **chapter** form, not the three-named-system form, so a fourth system established under one of those chapters is caught. A third formulation inside one subdivision is a worse defect than the one it cures.
- **B2. A predicted count is not a count.** §5.37 published *"§ 51.1-165 5 → 6"* and *"§ 51.1-162 6 → 11"* as projections; the mechanical count over §1 of the written body was **5 → 7** and **6 → 9**. Every citation figure in a sweep is counted over the live §1 after the write, never before it.
- **B3. A fatal finding is never resolved by drafting choice.** Where every available cure moves a loss between the survivor, the trust fund, the General Fund, the employing agency and the next applicant on the subdivision D 5 waiting list, the answers are drafted word for word and go on a card. Three of the four cures above were cards.
- **B4. Never re-stub a stub, and never move another run's section in the heartbeat it was written.** That is how a record gets double-stubbed and someone else's move gets dated to this revision. The apply kit asserts it.
- **B5. Derive every label from the live body; never carry a label in context.** Store revision numbers, sweep numbers, §6 numbers, §7 item numbers and the §0 zero count have each been wrong when predicted. The kit derives them and fails loudly.
- **B6. A stale self-description is a defect of the record.** §5's preamble, §6's preamble and §8.1's retained-note count have each been corrected after they stopped being true.
