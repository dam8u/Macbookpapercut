## 1. The bill

### Title

**A BILL to amend and reenact §§ 51.1-142.2 and 51.1-162 of the Code of Virginia and to amend the Code of Virginia by adding a section numbered 51.1-142.4, relating to the Virginia Retirement System; purchase of prior active duty military service credit by state employees; cost; pilot program; post-retirement employment; reports.**

### Enacting clause

**Be it enacted by the General Assembly of Virginia:**

**1. That §§ 51.1-142.2 and 51.1-162 of the Code of Virginia are amended and reenacted and that the Code of Virginia is amended by adding a section numbered 51.1-142.4 as follows:**

---

### § 51.1-142.2. Prior service or membership credit for certain members; service credit for accumulated sick leave.

**A.** Certain members may purchase credit for service as provided in this section.

**B. 1.** Any member in service may purchase service credit from the following categories of service or leave: (i) leave of absence for educational purposes that was previously approved by the member's employer, including unpaid educational leave as set forth in subsection D of § 22.1-296; (ii) leave of absence for a serious health condition of the member or of an immediate family member, all as defined in the Family and Medical Leave Act of 1993, 29 U.S.C. § 2601 et seq., as amended, and previously certified by the member's employer; (iii) up to one year of service credit per occurrence of leave for any unpaid leave of absence due to the birth, adoption, or death of a qualifying child, as defined in § 51.1-500; (iv) service as a full-time employee of another state, a public school system of another state, or a political subdivision of the Commonwealth or another state, as certified by such state, public school system, or political subdivision; (v) full-time service of a political subdivision of this state not credited to the member under an agreement as provided for in § 51.1-143.1, as certified by such political subdivision; (vi) full-time civilian service of the United States; (vii) full-time service at a private institution of higher education if the private institution is merged with a public institution of higher education and graduates of the private institution are then issued new degrees from the public institution; or (viii) any period of time when the member was employed part time or in a wage position by a participating employer and not otherwise eligible to participate in the retirement system because the member was not an employee as defined in § 51.1-124.3. However, no member in service shall be allowed to purchase more than a total of four years of service credit pursuant to this subdivision.

**2.** In addition to the service credit that may be purchased under subdivision 1, any member in service may purchase up to four years of service credit for prior active duty military service in the armed forces of the United States, provided that the discharge from a period of active duty status with the armed forces was not dishonorable. <u>The cost of service credit purchased under this subdivision by a member whose qualifying military service election has been accepted by the Board under § 51.1-142.4 shall be determined under subdivision 4.</u>

**3.** The service credit to be credited to a member under this subsection shall be calculated at the ratio of one year, or portion thereof, of service credit to one year, or portion thereof, of service purchased, except for employment service purchased under clause (viii) of subdivision 1, which shall be calculated at the ratio of one month of service credit for each 173 hours of service as certified by the employer.

<u>**4.** a. As used in this subdivision 4:</u>

<u>"Initial average compensation" means the annual average of the member's creditable compensation for the first 36 months of membership service earned by the member as a state employee, as determined by the Board from the records of the retirement system. If the member has earned fewer than 36 months of such service, "initial average compensation" means the annual average of the member's creditable compensation for all such service. Service credit purchased under this section, service credited under § 51.1-143.1, and service credited under subsection I shall be excluded in identifying such 36 months. If the records of the retirement system are insufficient to determine the member's creditable compensation for any month within such 36 months, the Board shall determine initial average compensation from the earliest 12 consecutive months of creditable compensation shown in those records, annualized, and the member may submit payroll records certified by the employer for the Board's consideration before the Board makes its determination.</u>

<u>"Limitation year" means the limitation year of the defined benefit plan under which the member is covered, determined under § 415 of the Internal Revenue Code and the regulations thereunder.</u>

<u>"Qualifying military service election" means an election to purchase service credit under subdivision 2 that the Board has accepted under § 51.1-142.4.</u>

<u>"Standard cost" means, for each year or portion thereof of service credit to be purchased, the amount the member would be required to pay for that year or portion thereof under this subsection if subdivision 4 did not apply.</u>

<u>"State agency" means a state agency as defined in subsection A of § 51.1-142.4.</u>

<u>"Statutory interest" means interest at the rate of six percent per annum, compounded annually.</u>

<u>"Total normal cost rate" means the rate, expressed as a percentage of creditable compensation for each year or portion thereof of service credit purchased, that the Board publishes and uses to determine the approximate normal cost under subdivision 3 for the retirement plan under which the member is covered. On July 1, 2026, that rate was 12.50 percent for a member who is not described in clause (i) or (ii) of subdivision A 1 of § 51.1-155 and 10.74 percent for a member who is so described. The total normal cost rate applicable to a qualifying military service election is the rate in effect on the date the member filed the complete application for the election.</u>

<u>b. For each year or portion thereof of service credit to be credited under a qualifying military service election, the member shall pay the lesser of (i) the product of the total normal cost rate and the member's initial average compensation, increased by statutory interest from the last day of the thirty-sixth month used to determine initial average compensation, or from the last day of the final month so used if fewer than 36 months were used, to the valuation date stated in the Board's certification under subsection D of § 51.1-142.4, or (ii) the standard cost determined as of that valuation date. No statutory interest shall accrue after the valuation date, and the amount determined under this subdivision shall not change by reason of the method by which, or the date on which, payment is made or completed.</u>

<u>c. The Board shall state in the certification required by subsection D of § 51.1-142.4 the amount determined under each clause of subdivision b, the valuation date, the total normal cost rate used, and the actuarial assumptions used to determine the standard cost. A member may, once and by written request filed before the member completes payment, require the Board to redetermine both amounts as of a valuation date that is not later than the completion date stated in that certification. The Board shall make the redetermination within 30 days after receiving the request and shall notify the member in writing, and the member shall pay the lowest amount determined under either clause of subdivision b on either valuation date.</u>

<u>d. A purchase made under a qualifying military service election is not subject to the requirement that a member purchase service credit, or enter into a purchase of service credit contract, within the first 24 months of the member's active service following his first date of hire or the final day of any applicable leave of absence.</u>

<u>e. Notwithstanding subsection G, the member shall pay no less than five percent of the amount determined under subdivision b from funds other than funds paid by an employer to or on behalf of the member for the purpose of making such payment. A state agency may pay the remainder of that amount only if the state agency has first adopted a written policy governing such payments that applies to all of its employees who are eligible members as defined in § 51.1-142.4, and the state agency shall file that policy with the Board before the Board accepts any election by its employees. The policy may state a maximum aggregate amount that the state agency will pay in a fiscal year and the order in which the state agency will apply that amount, provided that the order shall be stated in the policy, shall be fixed in advance of any application of the policy, and shall be either the order in which its employees file complete applications with the Board or an order determined by lot. The state agency shall publish the policy.</u>

<u>f. The Board shall not accept payment under a qualifying military service election unless the service credit to be purchased, as credited, and the payments to be made satisfy § 415 of the Internal Revenue Code and the regulations thereunder, as determined by the Board. The Board shall make any determination under this subdivision that relates to a payment no later than 30 days after the member tenders that payment, and in every case no later than 30 days after the completion date stated in the certification. If the Board determines that it may not accept all or part of a payment for the reason stated in this subdivision, the Board shall give the member written notice of that determination, of the amount the Board may accept, and of the reasons for the determination no later than 30 days after making it, and the member may (i) reduce the service credit to be purchased to the largest amount for which the Board may accept payment, subject to subsection C of § 51.1-142.4, (ii) defer payment of the amount the Board may not accept to the earliest limitation year in which the Board may accept it, if there is a limitation year in which the Board may accept it, or (iii) cancel the election under subdivision G 5 of § 51.1-142.4. Where the member elects under clause (ii), the Board shall extend the completion date stated in the certification to a date that is no earlier than the first day of that limitation year and no later than 12 months after that day, and shall recertify the election under subdivision D 6 of § 51.1-142.4, and the reservation shall not be released under subdivision D 7 of § 51.1-142.4 by reason of the member's failure to complete payment by the completion date previously stated. No completion date, as extended one or more times under this subdivision, shall be later than 24 months after the completion date first stated in the certification, except that this sentence does not prevent the extension of a completion date to the last day of the period allowed by this subdivision for making an election under clause (i), (ii), or (iii) of this subdivision, or to a date no later than 12 months after the first day of a limitation year under clause (ii) of this subdivision. Where the Board determines that there is no limitation year in which it may accept the amount, the Board shall state that determination and the reasons for it in the notice required by this subdivision, and the member may proceed under clause (i) or clause (iii) only. In making a determination under this subdivision the Board shall apply whichever of the limitations described in § 415(n)(1) of the Internal Revenue Code permits the Board to accept the greater amount. A determination under this subdivision is not an error of the Board for the purposes of subdivision G 4 of § 51.1-142.4, and the member may seek review of it under subsection M of that section. The member may make an election under clause (i), (ii), or (iii) of this subdivision no later than 30 days after the Board gives the notice required by this subdivision, a notice being given for the purposes of this subdivision as provided in subsection A of § 51.1-142.4, and a completion date that would otherwise fall within that period is extended to the last day of that period. Nothing in this subdivision or in § 51.1-142.4 limits any right of a member under Chapter 1223 of Title 10 of the United States Code, under 38 U.S.C. § 4318, or under subsection I.</u>

<u>Except as provided in subdivision 4,</u> ~~For~~ <u>for</u> each year or portion thereof to be credited at the time of purchase under this subsection, the member shall pay the approximate normal cost of the retirement plan under which the member is covered at the time of such purchase, as determined by the Board in its sole discretion. If the member does not purchase, or enter into a purchase of service credit contract for, the service made available in this subsection within the first 24 months of the member's active service following his first date of hire or the final day of any applicable leave of absence, as applicable, then, for each year or portion thereof to be credited at the time of purchase, the member shall pay the actuarial equivalent cost. To the extent the member becomes inactive during the 24 months following his first date of hire or the final day of any applicable leave of absence, such periods shall not be included in the 24 months of active service.

Except as otherwise required by Chapter 1223 of Title 10 of the United States Code, as amended, no service credit may be purchased under this section if it is included in the calculation of any retirement allowance received or to be received by the member from this or another retirement system, or if there is a balance in a defined contribution account that serves as a primary retirement account related to such service.

For purposes of this subsection, "active duty military service" means full-time service of at least 180 consecutive days in the United States Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, Coast Guard, or any other distinct branch of the armed services as established by the federal government, or reserve components thereof.


**C.** Any member in service may purchase all prior service credit for creditable service lost from ceasing to be a member under this chapter, as provided in § 51.1-128, because of the withdrawal of his accumulated contributions. For each year or portion thereof to be credited at the time of purchase under this subsection, the member shall pay the withdrawn amount to be purchased plus interest accrued daily and compounded annually from the date of withdrawal to the date of payment at the assumed rate of return established by the Board for the actuarial valuation of the retirement system that is in effect at the time of the purchase. The Board shall develop guidelines and procedures for administering this subsection.

**D.** Any member in service may purchase service credit for accumulated sick leave on his effective date of retirement based upon such sums as the employer may provide as payment for any unused sick leave balances. The cost of service credit purchased under this subsection shall be the actuarial equivalent cost of such service.

**E.** Any member receiving benefits under the Virginia Workers' Compensation Act (§ 65.2-100 et seq.) may, in a manner prescribed by the Board and prior to the effective date of retirement, purchase service that is not reported to the retirement system by the member's employer while the member is receiving such benefits.

For each year or portion thereof to be credited at the time of purchase under this subsection, the member shall pay the approximate normal cost of the retirement plan under which the member is covered, as determined by the Board in its sole discretion. If the member does not purchase, or enter into a purchase of service credit contract for, any service made available in this subsection within the first 24 months of the member's active service following his first date of hire or the final day of any applicable leave of absence, then, for each year or portion thereof to be credited at the time of purchase, the member shall pay the actuarial equivalent cost. To the extent the member becomes inactive during the 24 months following his first date of hire or the final day of any applicable leave of absence, such periods shall not be included in the 24 months of active service.

**F.** Payment may be made in a lump sum at the time of purchase or by payroll deduction. Any number of additional deductions may be permitted at any time. Should any deduction be terminated before the member purchases the entire period contracted for, the member shall be credited with the number of full or partial months of service for which full payment has been made. If any deduction is continued after the entire period has been purchased, the member shall be credited with no more than the amount of service for which he was eligible and for which he paid, and the excess amount deducted shall be refunded to the member.

**G.** Any employer may elect to pay an equivalent amount in lieu of all member contributions required of its employees for the purchase of service credit pursuant to this section. These contributions shall not be considered wages for purposes of Chapter 7 (§ 51.1-700 et seq.), nor shall they be considered salary for purposes of this chapter.

**H.** In any case where member and employer contributions, as required under this chapter, were not made because of an error in the payroll, personnel, or other classification system of an employer participating in the retirement system, service that has not been credited because of such error may be purchased on the following basis:

1. The most recent three years of service credit shall be purchased, using applicable member and employer contribution rates and creditable compensation in effect for such period, in a manner and at the cost prescribed by the Board; and

2. All other years of service credit shall be purchased by the employer at an actuarial equivalent cost.

**I.** Any member may receive credit at no cost for service rendered in the armed forces of the United States provided (i) the member was on leave of absence from a covered position, (ii) the discharge from a period of active duty with the armed forces was not dishonorable, (iii) the member has not withdrawn his accumulated contributions, (iv) the member is not disabled or killed while on leave without pay while performing active duty military service in the armed forces of the United States, and (v) the member reenters service in a covered position within one year after discharge from the armed forces. In order to receive such service, the member must complete such forms and other requirements as are required by the Board and the retirement system.

### <u>§ 51.1-142.4. Military service purchase pilot program for state employees.</u>

<u>**A.** As used in this section:</u>

<u>"Certified subsidy" means the amount certified by the Board under subsection D for an election, which shall be the amount by which (i) the actuarial present value, determined as of the valuation date stated in the certification, of the benefits payable under this chapter to the member and to the member's survivors and beneficiaries, taking account of the service credit to be purchased and of the member's retirement on the member's anticipated earliest qualifying retirement date, exceeds (ii) the expected value, taken over the distribution of the member's date of retirement projected as required by this definition, of the actuarial present value, determined as of the valuation date stated in the certification, of the benefits payable under this chapter to the member and to the member's survivors and beneficiaries without the service credit to be purchased, less (iii) the amounts payable by the member and by any state agency under subdivision B 4 of § 51.1-142.2. The Board shall determine each actuarial present value using the actuarial assumptions adopted by the Board for the most recent actuarial valuation of the retirement system. For the purposes of clause (ii), the Board shall project the member's date of retirement by applying, as annual probabilities over the member's remaining working lifetime, the rates of retirement in those actuarial assumptions, together with the rates of mortality in those assumptions and no other decrement. No rate shall be applied under clause (ii) as though the member's retirement on any date were certain, and the date of retirement projected under clause (ii) shall not be determined by reference to any date on which the member first becomes eligible for a service retirement allowance, whether reduced or unreduced and whether determined with or without the service credit to be purchased. The certified subsidy shall not be less than the amount by which the standard cost of the service credit to be purchased, determined as of the valuation date stated in the certification, exceeds the amounts payable by the member and by any state agency under subdivision B 4 of § 51.1-142.2. For the purposes of clause (i), the member's anticipated earliest qualifying retirement date is the first day of the first month following the month that includes the completion date stated in the certification. The Board shall redetermine the certified subsidy as of the member's effective date of retirement as required by subdivision G 1, and the amount so redetermined shall not exceed the amount last certified and reserved for the election.</u>

<u>"Complete application" means an application for an election under this section that contains every item of information and every certification that the Board requires by procedures adopted and published under subsection M. The Board shall not require under those procedures any item that is not necessary to determine the member's eligibility, the service credit the member may purchase under subsection C, the amount payable by the member under subdivision B 4 of § 51.1-142.2, or the certified subsidy.</u>

<u>"Earliest qualifying retirement date" means the first day of the month following the month in which the Board credits to the member all of the service credit purchased under the member's election. Where the Board credits that service credit as of a date stated in a request under subsection E, the month in which the Board credits it is the month that includes the date so stated.</u>

<u>"Election" means a qualifying military service election as defined in subdivision B 4 of § 51.1-142.2.</u>

<u>"Eligible member" means a member in service who (i) is a state employee, (ii) is covered under the retirement plan established by this chapter, whether or not the member is a person described in clause (i) or (ii) of subdivision A 1 of § 51.1-155, (iii) does not participate in the hybrid retirement program established by § 51.1-169, (iv) is eligible to purchase service credit for prior active duty military service under subdivision B 2 of § 51.1-142.2, (v) has not reached his unreduced retirement eligibility date, determined without the service credit to be purchased, on the date the member files a complete application, taking account of all creditable service for which the member is entitled to credit as of that date, (vi) would, if the member's maximum purchasable amount determined as of the date the member files that application were credited to the member on that date, reach his unreduced retirement eligibility date on or before the first day of the month following the month in which the member files that application, (vii) would, if the least of the member's maximum purchasable amount and the amount specified in clause (ii) of subsection C, each determined as of the date the member files that application, or, where no amount is specified in clause (ii) of subsection C, the member's maximum purchasable amount determined as of that date, were credited to the member on that date, reach his unreduced retirement eligibility date at least one month earlier than the date on which the member would reach his unreduced retirement eligibility date determined without that service credit, and (viii) would reach his unreduced retirement eligibility date, determined with the service credit to be purchased, on or before the first day of the month following the month in which that service credit is credited to the member. For the purposes of clause (vii) of this definition, a date is at least one month earlier than a second date if it is on or before the day, of the calendar month preceding the month in which that second date falls, that corresponds in number to the day of that second date, or, where that preceding month has no corresponding day, on or before the first day of the month in which that second date falls. "Eligible member" does not include a member of a retirement system established under Chapter 2 (§ 51.1-200 et seq.), Chapter 2.1 (§ 51.1-211 et seq.), or Chapter 3 (§ 51.1-300 et seq.), a teacher, an employee of a political subdivision, the Governor, the Lieutenant Governor, the Attorney General, a member of the General Assembly, or any person whose retirement allowance would be payable under subdivision B 2 of § 51.1-155.</u>

<u>"Final qualifying retirement date" means the date that is 12 months after the member's earliest qualifying retirement date, except that the Board shall approve a later date where the Board's own processing requirements, or any other cause not attributable to the member, make the member's retirement on or before the date that would otherwise apply under this definition impracticable. A later date approved under this definition shall be the first day of a month and shall be not more than three months after the date determined under the first sentence of this definition. The Board shall approve a later date on its own initiative or upon the written request of the member, which the member may file at any time before the date that would otherwise apply under this definition or before any later date approved under this definition; the Board shall act upon such a request, and shall give the member written notice of its determination and of the reasons for it, no later than 30 days after the request is filed; and the member may seek review of the determination under subsection M. The Board shall determine the member's earliest qualifying retirement date and final qualifying retirement date, and shall state both of them and the reasons for any later date approved under this definition, in writing to the member no later than 15 days after the Board credits to the member the service credit purchased under the member's election, and shall give the member written notice of any later date approved after that statement no later than 15 days after the approval. Where the member's final qualifying retirement date as extended under subdivision D 1 or as tolled under subsection M is not the first day of a month, the member's final qualifying retirement date is the first day of the next month. In no event shall a member's final qualifying retirement date, as extended under subdivision D 1 or as tolled under subsection M, be later than 12 months after the date that applies under the first sentence of this definition or, where a later date has been approved under this definition, after that later date, except that this sentence does not apply to an extension under subdivision D 1 by reason of a statement or notice given later than the time required by this definition, and the aggregate of all extensions so excluded shall not exceed 12 months. Where the running of a period has been tolled under subsection M and the member is prevented by the preceding sentence from retiring in a qualifying retirement after that tolling ends, the member's final qualifying retirement date is, notwithstanding the preceding sentence, the first day of the second month following the month in which the Board last acts upon a request the filing of which tolled that period, and in no event later than 12 months after the latest date the preceding sentence permits. Notwithstanding the two preceding sentences, where the date that would otherwise apply under this definition is earlier than the first day of the second month following the month in which the Board gives the statement required by this definition, the member's final qualifying retirement date is that first day. Where the Board does not act upon a request the filing of which tolled the running of the member's final qualifying retirement date under subsection M within the period this definition allows for acting upon it, and the date that would otherwise apply under this definition is earlier than the first day of the second month following the month in which that period expired, the member's final qualifying retirement date is, notwithstanding any other provision of this definition, that first day. Where the Board's action upon a request determines a member's final qualifying retirement date under this definition, the Board shall state that date in its decision upon the request, and where the Board does not do so it shall give the member written notice of that date no later than 15 days after it acts upon the request. Where the date that would otherwise apply under this definition is earlier than the first day of the second month following the month in which the Board gives a decision or notice required by the preceding sentence, the member's final qualifying retirement date is, notwithstanding any other provision of this definition, that first day.</u>

<u>"Involuntary separation" means an involuntary separation as defined in subsection B of § 2.2-3200. "Involuntary separation" does not include a separation for reasons related to the job performance or misconduct of the member or a voluntary resignation. The date of an involuntary separation is the date on which the member's employment terminates or, where the member is placed on leave without pay-layoff or equivalent status, the date of that placement, or, in the case of any other involuntary separation, the date on which the member ceases to occupy the position from which the member is separated.</u>

<u>"Maximum purchasable amount" means, with respect to a member and a date, the least of (a) the amount of service credit the member is eligible to purchase under subdivision B 2 of § 51.1-142.2 on that date, (b) the amount of service credit specified in clause (i) of subsection C on that date, and (c) the amount of service credit specified in clause (iii) of subsection C on that date. Clause (ii) of subsection C does not limit the maximum purchasable amount.</u>

<u>"Program" means the pilot program established by this section.</u>

<u>"Qualifying retirement" means a service retirement under § 51.1-153, or under subsection A or C of § 51.1-155.1, with an effective date of retirement that is the first day of a month, is no earlier than the member's earliest qualifying retirement date, and is no later than the member's final qualifying retirement date, the allowance for which retirement is calculated in accordance with subdivision A 1 of § 51.1-155 and is not reduced under subdivision A 2 or A 3 of that section, in each case determined as though no option had been elected under § 51.1-165 or § 51.1-165.01 and no lump-sum distribution had been paid under § 51.1-165.01. Where the Board's processing requirements delay the commencement of payment of the member's retirement allowance beyond the effective date of retirement, the allowance shall be payable retroactively to that effective date.</u>

<u>"Standard cost" means standard cost as defined in subdivision B 4 a of § 51.1-142.2. A reference in this section to the standard cost of an amount of service credit is a reference to the aggregate of the standard cost for each year or portion thereof of that service credit.</u>

<u>"State agency" means any agency, authority, board, bureau, commission, department, or institution of the Commonwealth, including a public institution of higher education and an agency of the legislative or judicial branch.</u>

<u>"Unreduced retirement eligibility date" means the earliest date on which the member may retire under § 51.1-153, or under subsection A or C of § 51.1-155.1, and receive a service retirement allowance that is calculated in accordance with subdivision A 1 of § 51.1-155 and is not reduced under subdivision A 2 or A 3 of that section, in each case determined as though no option had been elected under § 51.1-165 or § 51.1-165.01 and no lump-sum distribution had been paid under § 51.1-165.01. For the purposes of this section the member may retire under subsection A or C of § 51.1-155.1 only where the member has incurred an involuntary separation, or the member's office has been lawfully abolished, as the case may be, on or before the date as of which the member's unreduced retirement eligibility date is determined or, where this sentence is applied otherwise than in the determination of the member's unreduced retirement eligibility date, on or before the member's effective date of retirement. Where this section does not state a date as of which the member's unreduced retirement eligibility date is determined, that date is, for the purposes of the preceding sentence, the date on which the Board makes the certification, recertification, determination, statement, or other decision in which that date is applied.</u>

<u>For the purposes of this section and of subdivision B 4 f of § 51.1-142.2, a written statement, notice, or certification required of the Board is given on the date the Board mails it to the person to whom it is required to be given or transmits it electronically to an address that person has provided to the Board for that purpose, except that an electronic transmission completed after 5:00 p.m. is given on the following day. For the purposes of this section and of subdivision B 4 f of § 51.1-142.2, where the Board mails a written statement, notice, or certification, every period within which a person may or must act that runs from the giving of that statement, notice, or certification is extended by three days.</u>

<u>**B.** The Program is established. An eligible member may purchase service credit for prior active duty military service under subdivision B 2 of § 51.1-142.2 at the cost determined under subdivision B 4 of § 51.1-142.2 if, and only if, the Board has accepted the member's election under subsection D. No person whom the definition of "eligible member" in subsection A excludes, including a teacher, an employee of a political subdivision, and a member of a retirement system established under Chapter 2 (§ 51.1-200 et seq.), Chapter 2.1 (§ 51.1-211 et seq.), or Chapter 3 (§ 51.1-300 et seq.), is eligible to make an election under this section. For the avoidance of doubt, service credit purchased under an election is prior service purchased under § 51.1-142.2, is creditable service as defined in § 51.1-124.3, and shall be treated for all purposes of this chapter in the same manner as other service credit purchased under subdivision B 2 of § 51.1-142.2. Participation in the Program is voluntary, and no employer shall require, request, or induce any employee to make an election.</u>

<u>**C.** An eligible member may purchase under an election no more service credit than the least of (i) four years, less any service credit previously purchased by the member under subdivision B 2 of § 51.1-142.2; (ii) the number of months of creditable service the member needs in order to have reached his unreduced retirement eligibility date as of the filing date of the member's complete application, taking account of all creditable service for which the member is entitled to credit as of that date, any fraction of a month in the number so determined being counted as a whole month; and (iii) the number of years of creditable service the member has earned as a state employee. The Board shall determine each of these amounts no later than 30 days after the member's application is completed and shall state them, the filing date of the member's complete application, and the completion date, in the certification required by subsection D. The Board shall not accept an election for which the amount determined under clause (ii) of this subsection is zero. Where the amount determined under clause (ii) of this subsection is zero, the Board shall give the member written notice of that determination, of the reasons for it, of the member's rights under subsection B of § 51.1-142.2, and of the standard cost, determined as of the date of the notice, of the service credit, if any, the member is eligible to purchase under subdivision B 2 of § 51.1-142.2 as of that date, no later than 30 days after the member's application is completed, and the member may seek review of the determination under subsection M. Where no number of months of creditable service would cause the member to have reached his unreduced retirement eligibility date as of the filing date of the member's complete application, clause (ii) of this subsection does not limit the service credit the member may purchase, and the Board shall, in place of determining and stating an amount under that clause, determine, no later than 30 days after the member's application is completed, that no amount is specified in that clause and shall state that determination in the certification required by subsection D. A determination that no amount is specified in clause (ii) of this subsection is not a determination that the amount under that clause is zero. Where no amount is specified in clause (ii) of this subsection, the amount determined under that clause is not zero for the purposes of this subsection. The Board shall state in the report required by subsection J every failure to make a determination, to state a matter this subsection requires it to state, or to give a notice, required by this subsection within the period this subsection allows for it. Where the Board does not give the notice required by this subsection within that period, the member may seek review under subsection M of the Board's failure to give it at any time before the Board gives it, and the period within which the member may seek review of a determination under this subsection runs from the date the Board gives that notice.</u>

<u>**D. 1.** The Board shall not accept an election until the Board has, in writing, certified to the member and reserved the certified subsidy for that election under this subsection. The filing of a request for reconsideration under this subsection, and the filing of a request under the definition of "final qualifying retirement date" in subsection A, tolls the running of the member's final qualifying retirement date and of any period within which the member or applicant must act under this section or under subdivision B 4 f of § 51.1-142.2, until 30 days after the Board acts upon the request, except that (i) no second or subsequent request for reconsideration of the same determination or of the same failure, and no second or subsequent request under that definition, tolls any period under this section or under subdivision B 4 f of § 51.1-142.2, and (ii) the aggregate of all tolling under this subsection shall not exceed 120 days, excluding any period after the expiration of the 90 days allowed by this subsection and before the Board acts upon the request, provided that the aggregate of all periods so excluded shall not exceed 90 days. An application that is the subject of a request for reconsideration under this subsection retains its filing date for the purposes of subdivisions D 4 and D 5 until the Board acts upon the request, and the Board shall act upon the application no later than 15 days after it acts upon the request. Upon completion of a reconsideration under this subsection, the final determination issued by the Board shall constitute a case decision as defined in § 2.2-4001. A member, retiree, beneficiary, applicant, personal representative of a deceased member's estate, person who has claimed any benefit payable on the death of a member, person to whom this section requires a notice to be given in respect of a benefit payable on the death of a member, or state agency from whose appropriation an amount was paid on a member's behalf, aggrieved by, and claiming the unlawfulness of, such case decision, shall have a right to seek judicial review thereof in accordance with Article 5 (§ 2.2-4025 et seq.) of the Administrative Process Act. Nothing in this subsection limits the right of any person under § 2.2-4026 to seek judicial review of a case decision. Articles 3 (§ 2.2-4018 et seq.) and 4 (§ 2.2-4024) of the Administrative Process Act shall not apply to a reconsideration under this subsection.</u>

<u>3. The sentence of subsection B of § 2.2-4021 providing that a decision shall be deemed to be in favor of the named party, and the corresponding sentence of subsection C of that section, shall not apply to a determination of the Board under this section or to a reconsideration under subdivision 2.</u>

<u>**N.** Upon the settlement of a certified subsidy under subdivision G 1 or G 4, the Commonwealth shall pay the amount settled to the retirement system from an appropriation made for that purpose. The Board shall account for the amounts certified, reserved, settled, released, and paid under this section separately from all other assets and liabilities of the retirement system and shall state those amounts in the report required by subsection J.</u>

### § 51.1-162. Death before retirement.

**A.** If a member dies before retirement, and if no benefits are payable under subsection B, the amount of his accumulated contributions shall be paid to the designated beneficiary or to a surviving relative according to the order of precedence set forth in this section. This amount shall be reduced by the amount of any retirement allowance previously received by the member under this chapter or the abolished system. Each member shall designate who is to receive a refund of accumulated contributions credited to his account in the event of the death of the member prior to retirement. The designation must be made in a manner prescribed by the Board.

If no designation has been made, or the death of the designated person occurs prior to the death of the member and another designation has not been made, the proceeds shall be paid to the persons surviving at the death of the member in the following order of precedence:

First, to the spouse of the member;

Second, if no surviving spouse, to the children of the member and descendants of deceased children, per stirpes;

Third, if none of the above, to the parents of the member;

Fourth, if none of the above, to the duly appointed executor or administrator of the estate of the member;

Fifth, if none of the above, to other next of kin of the member entitled under the laws of the domicile of the member at the time of his death.

**B.** To the extent required by § 401(a)(37) of the Internal Revenue Code, as amended or renumbered, and the regulations thereunder applicable to governmental plans, if a member dies in service, including a member performing active duty military service in the armed forces of the United States, and if no benefits are payable under subsection C of this section, a retirement allowance shall be paid to the person or persons designated as provided in subsection A of this section if the person is the member's (i) surviving spouse, (ii) minor child, or (iii) parent(s). If no designation has been made, or if the death of the designated person occurs prior to the death of the member and another designation has not been made, a retirement allowance shall be paid in the following order of precedence to the member's (a) surviving spouse, (b) minor children, or (c) parent(s). The retirement allowance shall be paid to the first person qualifying in the orders of precedence set out in this subsection. If more than one minor child survives the deceased member, the allowance shall be divided among them in a manner determined by the Board. If more than one parent survives the deceased member, the allowance shall be divided among them in a manner determined by the Board. The retirement allowance shall be continued during the lifetime of the person or in the case of a minor child until the child dies or attains the age of majority, whichever occurs first. The retirement allowance shall equal the decreased retirement allowance that would have been payable under the joint and survivor option so that the same amount would be continued to such person after the member's death. If the member dies prior to his fifty-fifth birthday, then, for purposes of this subsection, the member shall be presumed to be age 55 on his date of death. However, if the member dies in service prior to his sixtieth birthday and is (1) a person who becomes a member on or after July 1, 2010, (2) a member who does not have at least 60 months of creditable service as of January 1, 2013, or (3) a member of the hybrid retirement program described in § 51.1-169, then, for purposes of this subsection, the member shall be presumed to be age 60 on his date of death. When determining the allowance that would have been payable to the member had the member retired on the date of his death, the provisions of subdivision A 4 of § 51.1-155 shall not apply. If the person elects in writing, the amount of the member's accumulated contributions or lump sum payment shall be paid to him exclusively, in lieu of any other benefits under this section. This amount shall be reduced by the amount of any retirement allowance previously received by the member under this chapter.

**C.** If a member dies in service from a cause compensable under the Virginia Workers' Compensation Act (§ 65.2-100 et seq.), a retirement allowance shall be paid to the member's surviving spouse. If no compensation is finally awarded under the Virginia Workers' Compensation Act due to legal proceedings or otherwise resulting in settlement from the persons causing such death, the Virginia Workers' Compensation Commission shall determine whether the member's death was from a cause compensable under the Virginia Workers' Compensation Act. If the member leaves no surviving spouse or the surviving spouse dies, any minor children of the deceased member shall be paid an allowance until the children die or attain the age of majority, whichever occurs first. If more than one minor child survives the deceased member, the allowance shall be divided in a manner determined by the Board. If the deceased member leaves neither surviving spouse nor minor child, the allowance, divided in a manner determined by the Board, shall be paid to the member's parents during their lives.

The retirement allowance payable hereunder to a qualifying survivor shall be the annual amount which when added to the compensation payable under the Virginia Workers' Compensation Act for the death of the member equals 50 percent of the member's average final compensation if the survivor does not qualify for death benefits under the provisions of the Social Security Act in effect on the date of the death of the member. If the survivor qualifies for death benefits under the provisions of the Social Security Act in effect on the date of the death of the member, the allowance payable from the retirement system when added to the compensation payable under the Virginia Workers' Compensation Act shall equal thirty-three and one-third percent of the member's average final compensation.

Any beneficiary entitled to the entire amount of a retirement allowance under the provisions of this subsection as a result of the death of a member shall be entitled to waive his rights to the allowance by written notification to the Board within ninety days after the death of the member<u>, except as subdivision G 4 of § 51.1-142.4 provides,</u> in order to make available a retirement allowance under the provisions of subsection B of this section.
