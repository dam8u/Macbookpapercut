### §6.0-69 Ambiguities resolved in store revision 69 — and one residual declined with its reason

1. **Whether to enact the Director's approved words as given.** f1-b's words are the reviewer's own, quoted verbatim on the card and approved verbatim. **They are reproduced word for word and two conforming limbs are added to them.** The test I applied is the one this document has applied to every approved sentence: a change that makes the approved words operate as approved is conformance and belongs in the text with a note; a change that moves a cost or settles a question the card put to the Director is not mine to make. Both limbs are of the first kind, both are recorded at §0.0-69 item 1, and **either can be reversed in one substitution.**

2. **Why the exception is confined to a retirement *"that is not a qualifying retirement."*** The approved words were confined only by *"for the purposes of this subdivision."* Subdivision G 4 uses the phrase *"a retirement before the date of death"* for **two** populations — the member who retired in a retirement that is not a qualifying retirement, which is the carve-out's own subject, and the member who retired in a qualifying retirement, who is the subject of the branch's second condition. **Unconfined, the exception would have reached the second and pulled a retiree out of § 51.1-163 and into the death branch.** That is the class [MOO-161](/MOO/issues/MOO-161)'s F1 rated **fatal**, and the Policy Director's standing direction of 12 October 2026 exists to keep this sentence off it. The confinement is five words and it removes the argument rather than winning it.

3. **Why the second limb is needed and is not an enlargement.** The approved words make a statement about a **noun** — *"is not a retirement before the date of death."* The condition that lets the death branch operate is a **verb**: *"where clause (i) of this subdivision applies and **the member has not retired** before the date of death."* A reader could hold that a member whose effective date fell before her death *"has retired"* however the noun is defined. **The limb therefore does not widen the answer; without it the answer does not reach the class the card described.** It is drafted in the chapter's own formulation — *"shall not be treated as having"* — which subsection E and subdivision G 5 already use, so the act has one formulation for one concept and not two.

4. **One residual of f1-b, declined with its reason.** A disability retiree **already in payment** whose effective date the Board *re-determines* after her death is, on the words, a member whose effective date the Board determined after the death. **I have not drafted against it.** The better reading is that an effective date is *determined* once, when the retirement is approved, and that a later correction is not that determination; and the only drafted cure is **f1-a's** limb — *"and a retirement allowance has become payable to the member under that retirement"* — which is a limb of a form the Director considered and **did not** select. Taking it now would be re-deciding the card. It is named to the next counsel pass as the first thing to attack, and it is one substitution if the Director wants it.

5. **Why the non-coincident branch is a clause of the greater-of sentence and not a sentence of its own.** That sentence now contains **five** references to *"the preceding sentence"* where it contained two, and every one of them means the sentence that determines the benefit under § 51.1-162. **A new sentence would have given the references inside it a new antecedent — the greater-of sentence — and the branch would have been about the wrong benefit.** The semicolon keeps one referent for all five. **Any further amendment of this branch must stay inside that sentence for the same reason**, and that is recorded as a standing direction rather than left in this note.

6. **Two pronouns made express.** *"The benefit **it** provides"* becomes *"the benefit **that subdivision** provides"*, because *"it"* had two candidate antecedents in a sentence that decides who is paid; and the payee tail becomes *"shall pay it to **that person or those persons**"*, which is the set the new condition has just identified, in place of *"the person to whom the provision under which the greater benefit is determined requires it to be paid."* **Neither changes the rule.** The second is shorter because the condition now does the work the old formula did.

7. **No section is named to displace one.** The non-coincident branch directs the Board to pay what subdivision C 2 of § 51.1-165 provides and not to pay the benefit determined under the preceding sentence. **It names no section as disapplied and adds no *"notwithstanding."*** [MOO-96](/MOO/issues/MOO-96)'s F-4 — that every section this act names to displace is a section a reader of that section learns nothing from — is therefore not reopened, and the act's two existing displacements are not enlarged.

8. **Where the eligibility bar goes inside subsection B.** Between the sentence that makes an accepted election the only route to a purchase and the avoidance-of-doubt sentence: **grant, then bar, then clarification, then the voluntariness rule.** The alternative was the end of the subsection, after voluntariness, which puts two sentences between the grant and the bar on it. The bar is moved **word for word** and nothing but its place changes; the net character count of the move is **zero**.

9. **Subsection L is not rewritten.** What is left is the local-mandate disclaimer, which reads as a complete sentence on its own and was the whole of the subsection before the Director's answer f10-b put the bar there. **The smallest change that achieves the agreed policy is to take one sentence out, and that is what is done.**

10. **f11-authority is not drafted, and that is the answer and not an omission.** The Director's selection is an instruction to harden the *"complete in itself"* authority before the drafting request goes to Division of Legislative Services. It is a research question about Supreme Court of Virginia authority, the reviewer offered in terms to run it down, and **there is no provision to enact for it.** It is commissioned as an issue.

11. **The item-130 annotation is replaced and not supplemented.** Store revision 67's annotation records route (b) as **held**. The Director has now **declined** it. A record that carries both, with the stale one first, is worse than a record that carries one: the rule this document has applied since store revision 62 is that **a stale self-description is a defect of the record.** The annotation now states the declination, its ground, the adoption of route (c) in [MOO-164](/MOO/issues/MOO-164), and the one thing the Director's note corrects in my own inventory — that the subdivision D 4 / D 5 retention and the 15-day duty are **one sentence, and it is the sentence after clause (ii)**, so no amendment confined to clause (ii) could ever have reached either.

12. **Items 132, 133 and 134 are moved and not renumbered.** They were written after the *"### 7.1 Retained record"* subsection, so the live list stopped at 131 for any reader who trusted the caption. **Moving them is a correction of place and not of content:** not one word of any of the three changes, §7 is asserted contiguous **1 to 134** before and after, and every *"§7 item n"* reference in this document and in the retained record resolves exactly as it did. **This is the second occurrence in this document** — store revision 37 did it with items 59, 60 and 61 — so the kit now asserts that the last item of the live list precedes the §7.1 heading.

13. **What the non-selection of two answers does not decide.** **f12-ninety** is not enacted, so the § 51.1-162 C waiver floor stays at the thirtieth day after the notice is given, and the beneficiary noticed on day 85 still gets thirty days where a timely-noticed beneficiary got ninety. **f3-operative** is not enacted, so a member who elected the straight life option under subdivision A 1 of § 51.1-165 — *"an increased retirement allowance in lieu of any death benefits"* — may still have no death benefit for the subsidy to buy. **Both remain drafted word for word at §7 item 132 and neither is closed by this revision.** What is closed is f1-a, f1-c, f4-b and f4-c, each by the selection of its alternative, and each is marked as closed by selection rather than deleted.
