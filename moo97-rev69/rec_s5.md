**Direction 2 — what cites what this revision changed.** Every word touched is inside **new § 51.1-142.4**, which no section of the Code cites, because it does not yet exist. Nothing in §1 cites subsection L, and nothing in §1 or §2 cites subdivision G 4's carve-out sentence or the greater-of sentence by any formulation other than the internal *"preceding sentence"* references the revision preserves. **§ 51.1-142.2 and § 51.1-162, the act's two amended sections, are untouched by this revision**, so the Article IV, Section 12 reproduction obligation is not engaged by anything in it.

**Direction 3 — the internal positional references, which are the ones this revision could have broken.**

1. **Five *"preceding sentence"* references in the greater-of sentence, where there were two.** All five mean the sentence that determines the benefit under § 51.1-162. The non-coincident branch is joined by a semicolon rather than made a sentence of its own **so that none of them acquires a new antecedent.** Counted inside that one sentence, which goes from 785 to 1,288 characters: 2 → 5, and §1's total 19 → 22, so **every new use is inside it.**
2. **The sentence that follows the carve-out.** *"In the case of death, the Board shall credit the service credit purchased"* is unchanged and still follows; f1-b lengthens the carve-out sentence and adds no sentence, so no *"next sentence"* or *"preceding sentence"* reference in subdivision G 4 moves relative to its target. §1's line count is unchanged at 221 and **three** lines differ.
3. **Subsection B's internal order.** The bar lands between *"…has accepted the member's election under subsection D."* and *"For the avoidance of doubt, …"*. The avoidance-of-doubt sentence refers to *"service credit purchased under an election"* and not to any preceding sentence positionally, so the insertion separates nothing.

**Conforming amendments elsewhere in the Code: none.** No section outside the act is amended, cited for the first time, or deprived of a citation by this revision. **The two *"notwithstanding"* displacements in subdivision G 4 are unchanged and still travel in the drafting request to Division of Legislative Services** — subdivision C 1 of § 51.1-165 and subsection F of § 51.1-165.01 learn nothing from their own text, and **f11-authority is the Director's instruction to harden the authority for exactly that.** A drafter's note is not enacted.
