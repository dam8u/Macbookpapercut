**Bounded authority question, commissioned by the Policy Director out of [MOO-97](/MOO/issues/MOO-97) §7 item 132, answer (6) (f11-authority), approved 12 October 2026.** Self-contained: everything you need is below.

## The question

New § 51.1-142.4 (a new section added by the draft bill, not yet introduced) contains **two operative displacements of provisions in sections the act does not amend**, both inside subdivision G 4:

1. *"Notwithstanding subdivision C 1 of § 51.1-165 …"* — displacing that subdivision's routing of a post-notification, pre-effective-date death to § 51.1-163.
2. *"Notwithstanding so much of subsection F of § 51.1-165.01 as requires benefits to be paid in accordance with § 51.1-163 …"* — displacing that limb only.

**Neither § 51.1-165 nor § 51.1-165.01 is amended by the act, so neither carries any signal of the displacement on its own face.** A reader of § 51.1-165 C 1 learns nothing about it.

**The prior pass ([MOO-189](/MOO/issues/MOO-189) F11) reported this as constitutionally sound** — Article IV, Section 12 of the Constitution of Virginia requires reenactment at length of a section that is *amended*, and an act complete in itself that supersedes prior law by implication is not an amendment in that sense — **but expressly recorded one limit: it verified the constitutional text and did not locate controlling Supreme Court of Virginia authority on the "complete in itself" doctrine within the pass, and offered to run it down.** The Director has asked for that authority.

## What is wanted

1. **The controlling Virginia authority on the "complete in itself" doctrine** under Va. Const. art. IV, § 12 (and its predecessor provisions) — cases, with citations, and the holding each actually supports.
2. **Whether a *"notwithstanding [another section]"* clause in a new section is treated as an amendment of the named section** for art. IV, § 12 purposes on that authority, and whether displacing *part* of a subsection (displacement 2 above) is treated differently from displacing a whole subdivision.
3. **Whether the authority distinguishes** a displacement that is express and names the section (ours) from one that operates by implication, and whether naming the section helps or hurts.
4. **If the authority is adverse or unclear:** what the cheapest cure is, stated as drafting direction — a conforming amendment to § 51.1-165 and/or § 51.1-165.01 (which would add them to the title line and the first enactment clause and oblige reproduction at length), a reference clause inside those sections, or something else.
5. **What must travel in the drafting request to Division of Legislative Services** either way. A drafter's note is not enacted, so anything a DLS drafter must know has to be in the request.
