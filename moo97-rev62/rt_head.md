## Why this pass is narrow and why it matters

**Subdivision G 4's death branch has now had four cures for one defect, and three of the four drew a fatal finding from the next pass.** [MOO-126](/MOO/issues/MOO-126) F1 was fatal on store revision 45. [MOO-154](/MOO/issues/MOO-154) F1 was fatal on store revision 54. [MOO-161](/MOO/issues/MOO-161) F1 was fatal on store revision 58. [MOO-169](/MOO/issues/MOO-169) F1 was fatal on store revision 61 — and **E1 below is the fourth attempt.** It is a *widening*, where the last one was a narrowing, and the reviewer of store revision 61 said in terms that the subdivision should not go to Division of Legislative Services in that form.

**This revision also enacts fourteen of MOO-169's findings and all seven answers the Policy Director selected on 12 October 2026.** None of the twenty-one substitutions has had an adversarial pass.

## What to attack, in order

1. **E1 — the fourth cure of the death branch.** The condition is now *"and the member has not retired before the date of death."* Does it reach the separated pre-filing member MOO-169 F1 was about? Does it keep the retiree out, so that MOO-161 F1's forfeiture cannot return? **Is there a third class it reaches that neither prior form did** — in particular a member who has retired in a *qualifying* retirement with a retroactive effective date under subdivision D 1's own retroactivity sentence, or a member approved for a disability retirement allowance who then dies?
2. **E9 — the widened subdivision M 2 class, in both lists.** Store revision 61 narrowed it because *"other person to whom this section requires a notice to be given"* reached the public through subsection K. The new words carry a subject-matter boundary: *"in respect of a benefit payable on the death of a member."* **Does that boundary hold?** Name anyone it lets in whom it should not, and anyone it still shuts out. Separately: dropping the adverse-determination gate makes *"person who **has** claimed"* self-certifying — **can a stranger manufacture standing by writing to the Board?**
3. **E7 — one window for an incomplete certification, and M 2's second sentence expressly disapplied to it.** Does the disapplication strand anything? Is 30 days from the certification a window the member can actually use, and if not, is the residue a defect or a policy setting?
4. **E11 — the charging rule for a reinstated settled amount.** I declined half the fix direction on the ground that subdivision G 4's notice sentence already says *"every reinstatement under this subdivision"* and so already carried the excess machinery. **Check that reading.** If it is wrong, the $3,000,000 bar can be over-committed silently and the finding is serious.
5. **The five enacted propositions of law in subdivision D 1** — E2, E3, E4, E5, E6. The Board must state these in writing **before it may accept the member's money**. Three of them were wrong last revision and three were wrong the revision before. **Read each against the live Code text and tell me if any of them is still wrong.** E3 restates § 51.1-165 C 2 with its identity condition; E4 restates § 51.1-162 B's designation requirement; E5 adds § 51.1-162 C's *"in service"*; E6 adds § 51.1-162 A's payee rule; E2 narrows the displacement of § 51.1-165 C 1 and § 51.1-165.01 F to their routing limbs only.
6. **E18 — f7-toll, which overrides a period stated in § 51.1-162 C, a section this act does not amend.** Is an override in the new section effective without amending § 51.1-162? Does the 30-day floor interact badly with § 51.1-162 C's own machinery or with the § 51.1-162 B election, which has no period?
7. **E16 — the greater-of's measure and payee.** I have already carded the residue that § 51.1-163's measure has no content in a pre-effective-date death. **Is actuarial equivalence as of the date of death a performable instruction at all** when one side is a lump sum whose own measure is indeterminate, and does the payee rule create a conflict where the two provisions name different people?
8. **E20 — f10-b in subsection L.** I enacted it in the chapter form because the definition of *"eligible member"* already excludes Chapter 2, 2.1 and 3 members. **Is the restatement harmless, or does a redundant exclusion in subsection L invite the argument that the definition means something narrower?**
