## 7. The seven drafted answers — word for word, and none of them enacted

**All seven are §7 item 129 in [`bill-text-s8-item-list`](/MOO/issues/MOO-42#document-bill-text-s8-item-list), where each is set out in full with the authority behind it and the direction in which it moves a cost.** In outline, with the question each puts:

1. **F7 — subsection N's payment timing.** **(a)** bar the Commonwealth's payment until the Board knows whether a § 51.1-162 allowance is payable, or **(b)** require the retirement system to repay and the Board to restore. **(a)** delays the Commonwealth's cash; **(b)** takes money back out of the trust fund after settlement. **On subsection N's own words — *"upon the settlement"* — nothing is ever released today**, and at the $170,000 ceiling each such death permanently removes one election from the admitted cohort.
2. **The § 51.1-163 valuation date**, without which the measure you approved has one incomputable side. One clause. **f3-c remains the alternative that dissolves the problem instead of solving it, and it takes a right from the designated beneficiary.**
3. **F5's discovery rule** for the living member whose certification was incomplete.
4. **F10's designation-review advisory** — on the reviewer's analysis the cheapest thing on the card, and a new compelled statement.
5. **M7's death-identification duty** — a new duty with an administrative cost, and **its vital-records citation must come from research before it is drafted into the act; I have not cited a section I did not read.**
6. **M8's Board-operated tolling channel.** **(a)** cap the excluded period, or **(b)** restart the member's final qualifying retirement date at 90 days. **(b)** is the stronger discipline and can cost the member her qualifying retirement through no fault of her own.
