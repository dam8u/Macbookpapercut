### 0.0-62 Store revision 62 — [MOO-169](/MOO/issues/MOO-169)'s counsel pass disposed and the Policy Director's seven answers of 12 October 2026 enacted. Twenty-one substitutions in five provisions of § 51.1-142.4

**Cut and applied on [MOO-97](/MOO/issues/MOO-97). Record: [`moo169-disposition`](/MOO/issues/MOO-97#document-moo169-disposition).** Sweep §5.41; drafter's note §6.0-62; the carded answers §7 item 129. **No figure moves, no designator is renumbered, no section joins or leaves the act, no defined term is added, and the title line does not change.** §1 goes from 117,980 to 120,888 characters and **five lines of §1 differ** — subdivision D 1, subdivision G 4, subsection L, subdivision M 2 and subsection N.

**The fatal finding, cured by taking the window out again.** [MOO-169](/MOO/issues/MOO-169) F1 held that store revision 61's confinement of the death-benefit sentence to the notification window in subdivision D 1 *moved* MOO-161 F1's injury rather than curing it: a member who is out of service after an involuntary separation, whom subdivision D 1 expressly tells she need not file her § 51.1-153 notice early, and who dies before filing, fell outside the window — so the in-service waiver never reached her, § 51.1-162 B was unavailable, and § 51.1-162 A paid her survivor a refund of contributions while the Commonwealth settled and paid the certified subsidy on the date of her death. **The window is deleted and the sentence is conditioned instead on the member not having retired before the date of death**, which is the carve-out clause (i) already uses in its own second sentence. That restores the scope of answer **(f)** as the Director approved it on 12 October 2026 and as it stood at store revisions 54, 58 and 60; **the confinement was this drafter's, not the Director's**, and §6.0-62 item 1 says so. **F2 is cured by the same deletion** — there is no longer a cross-reference to a notification *"required by subdivision D 1"* for the Board to read narrowly.

**Three statements of law the act compels the Board to make before it may take a member's money were wrong, and are now right.** § 51.1-165 **C 2** does not provide a greater-of at large: it pays in accordance with § 51.1-163 and switches to § 51.1-162 B **only where the contingent annuitant and the § 51.1-162 B payee are the same person or persons**, and the certification's invented words *"each payable to the person entitled to it under the provision under which it is determined"* described a mechanism the Code does not have (F9). § 51.1-162 **B** does not pay on survival alone: it pays a **designated** spouse, minor child or parent, or, absent a designation, in the order of precedence it states — so a member who designated her sister in 2009 and is survived by her mother gets nothing from the purchase, and the certification told her otherwise (F10). § 51.1-162 **C** requires death **in service** (M6). § 51.1-162 **A**'s payee rule is now stated too (m3), and the displacement of § 51.1-165 C 1 and § 51.1-165.01 F is described as what it is — a displacement of their routing to § 51.1-163 only, leaving their first sentences, which void the member's option election, in force (M5).

**The remedy for an incomplete certification now works for the person most affected by it.** It reached only the six propositions, not the certification's earlier content sentence; it ran the member's clock from a death notice she will never receive; it named a category — *"a failure of the Board"* — that subdivision M 2 does not contain; and it collided with M 2's second sentence, which deems a certification a determination and had already closed her 30 days. **All four are cured in one substitution** (F5, m2): every matter the subdivision requires, the M 2 category named exactly, the member's period run from the certification she actually received, the death-case person's period left where it was, and M 2's second sentence expressly disapplied to such a failure. M 2's own third window — *"within the period provided by the provision that requires the matter to be stated"* — was **empty everywhere but subdivision D 1**, and now carries the chapter's own 30 days with a longer-period rider (F6).

**The reconsideration class is widened, with the boundary store revision 61 was reaching for.** Standing turned on the Board having already decided against the claimant, so the Board could defeat review by never deciding — and because M 2 makes the case decision the product of reconsideration, exclusion from reconsideration foreclosed the circuit court as well (F3). The same narrowing had stripped the **court-appointed conservator of a minor beneficiary** whom the same revision had made the addressee of the death notice (F4). Both of M 2's class lists now name *"person who **has** claimed any benefit payable on the death of a member"* — with no adverse-determination gate — and *"person to whom this section requires a notice to be given **in respect of a benefit payable on the death of a member**."* **This is not a reversal of store revision 61's reasoning**: what was wrong with the open formula was that it had no subject-matter boundary and so reached the public through subsection K; the new words carry one. §6.0-62 item 3.

**The $3,000,000 bar is made arithmetically honest in two of the three places the pass attacked.** A reinstated settled amount is now **charged against the limitations in subdivision D 2** notwithstanding that the amount available is thereby exceeded, so commitments can no longer stand at $3,148,000 under a bar subdivision D 2 calls self-executing (F8); the release carries a *"to the extent it has not already been released and not reinstated"* rider, so it cannot be required twice of the same amount (M3); and the stranded words *"together with any contingency amount released with it"*, which pointed at a release that never carries a contingency amount, are deleted (M2). **The third place is not cured and is the Director's**: subsection N fixes no time for payment while subdivision G 4 settles the subsidy on the date of death, so whether a subsidy that buys no benefit comes back to the cap is an unregulated administrative choice (F7, §7 item 129 answer 1).

**Two corrections to the pass itself, both stated at §6.0-62.** F8's fix direction asked for the general reinstatement sentence's *"charging rule and excess consequences"* to be adopted by reference. **Only the charging rule was missing.** Subdivision G 4's notice sentence already speaks of *"every reinstatement under this subdivision"*, so the notice to the Chairmen, the notice to every waiting-list applicant, the subdivision D 3 confirmation, the certification of the excess to the Governor, the subsection J line and *"shall accept no further election until the excess is eliminated"* already applied to a reinstatement under this release. Adopting them again by reference would have been a second formulation of one concept. And F1's fix direction offered *"the member dies before the effective date of any retirement of the member"*; that is **vacuous for the member who never retired at all**, who is the largest part of the class the finding is about, so the enacted words are the negative the subdivision already uses.

**The Director's seven, as enacted.** The greater-of's **measure** (actuarial equivalence as of the date of death, on the Board's assumptions then in use) and its **payee** (the person the provision producing the greater benefit requires to be paid) — f3-a and f3-b, cumulative, in one sentence. The **§ 51.1-162 C waiver period**, which the act's own clocks could destroy before the Board's notice of it arrived, now cannot expire before the thirtieth day after a late notice — f7-toll, **and it is the act's first operative override of a period stated in a section this act does not amend**. A **further notice with a fresh review period** where the Board later determines an amount differently — f14-supp. **Lateness reviewable for 30 days after the Board cures it** — m2-lateness. The express statement that **a § 51.1-162 death benefit is not charged against the subdivision D 2 limitations** — f11-disclose. And the **confinement of the Program to Chapter 1 members** — f10-b, enacted in subsection L **in the chapter form the definition of *"eligible member"* already uses**, not in the three-named-system form the card carried; see §6.0-62 item 12, because the scope decision the Director made was already the law of the act and the sentence is a restatement, parallel to subsection L's existing sentence on teachers.

**Store revision 62 is cut and applied on the live base at document revision `3efccfe2` (store revision 61).** Every one of the twenty-one FIND strings was asserted to occur exactly its expected number of times in §1 before any substitution — twenty of them once, the M 2 class list twice, once in each of its two lists. **Every citation figure in §5.41 is a count taken over §1 of the body that was written, not a prediction**; the rule that a predicted count is not a count was set at §5.37 and holds here.
