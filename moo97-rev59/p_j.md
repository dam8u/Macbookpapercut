<u>**J. 1.** The Board shall report, no later than December 1 of each year in which this section is in effect and no later than December 1 of each year thereafter until the first December 1 following the later of (a) the date on which every election the Board accepted under this section has been settled under subdivision G 1 or G 4, cancelled under subdivision D 6 or G 5, or determined under subdivision G 2 or G 3, or has had its reservation released under subdivision D 7 without any service credit having been credited to the member, and (b) the date of the last rescission under subdivision G 4 that has occurred on or before the date stated in clause (a), to the Governor, to the Director of the Department of Planning and Budget, and to the Chairmen of the House Committee on Appropriations and the Senate Committee on Finance and Appropriations. The report shall state, for the reporting period and cumulatively: the number of complete applications filed; the number of elections accepted, denied, and wait-listed; the amounts certified, reserved, settled, released, and paid; the number of members who retired in a qualifying retirement and the number who did not; the service credit purchased and the amounts paid by members and by employers; the number of determinations rescinded and the service credit restored under subdivision G 4; the number of elections settled under subdivision G 4 by reason of an error of the Board and the amounts so settled; every amount by which a reservation reinstated under subdivision G 4 caused a limitation in subdivision D 2 to be exceeded; the variance between the certified subsidies and the actuarial cost to the retirement system as determined in the most recent actuarial valuation; every failure of the Board to make a determination, or to give a notice, within a period required by this section, stated separately for each provision; and, to the extent not otherwise required by this subdivision, every other matter this section requires the Board to state in that report. Where a rescission under subdivision G 4 occurs after the Board has made the last report required by this subdivision, the Board shall make one further report, limited to that rescission and its consequences, no later than the first December 1 following the rescission.</u>
