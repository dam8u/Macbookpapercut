<u>**E.** The member shall complete payment of the amount determined under subdivision B 4 of § 51.1-142.2 by the completion date stated in the Board's certification, which shall be no later than 12 months after the date the Board accepts the election. Payment may be made by any method authorized by subsection F of § 51.1-142.2 and by the Board's procedures, provided that the aggregate of all payments shall equal the amount stated in the certification and no additional amount shall be charged by reason of the method or the timing of payment. The Board shall credit the purchased service credit to the member only upon receipt of payment in full, and shall do so within 30 days after receipt of payment in full. The member may request in writing, before or together with the final payment, that the Board credit the service credit as of the last day of a month stated in the request that is not later than 30 days after the date on which the member expects to make the final payment; the Board shall credit the service credit as of the date so stated if payment in full is received on or before that date, and in every other case shall credit it within 30 days after receipt of payment in full. In every case the Board shall give the member written notice of the date as of which it credits the service credit no later than five days after it credits it. No service credit purchased under an election shall be credited before payment in full is received, except as subdivision G 4 provides in the case of the member's death, and, except for the purposes of the definition of "eligible member" in subsection A, no member shall be treated as having reached his unreduced retirement eligibility date by reason of an election before payment in full is received.</u>
