Subdivisions 2 and 3 shall not apply, and the service credit purchased under the election shall vest at the cost determined under subdivision B 4 of § 51.1-142.2, if the member, after the Board accepts the election, (i) dies, (ii) is approved for a disability retirement allowance under this chapter, or (iii) is unable to retire in a qualifying retirement by reason of an error of the Board. For the purposes of clause (iii) of this subdivision, a member who retires at a time when the Board has not given a statement or notice of the member's final qualifying retirement date required by the definition of "final qualifying retirement date" in subsection A, and the time allowed by that definition for giving that statement or notice has expired, is unable to retire in a qualifying retirement by reason of an error of the Board; and a member whose final qualifying retirement date passes at a time when the Board has not given such a statement or notice and the time allowed by that definition for giving it has expired is unable to retire in a qualifying retirement by reason of an error of the Board. For the purposes of the preceding sentence, a statement or notice of the member's final qualifying retirement date required by that definition does not include the notice of the Board's determination upon a request, and of the reasons for it, that that definition requires to be given no later than 30 days after the request is filed, and the remedies of the member for a failure of the Board to give that notice are those provided by subdivision D 1, by that definition, and by subsection M. Clause (i) of this subdivision does not apply where the member has retired in a retirement that is not a qualifying retirement before the date of death. In the case of death, the Board shall credit the service credit purchased and shall pay any benefit payable to the member's survivor or beneficiary. Notwithstanding the crediting required by the preceding sentence, where the member dies before completing payment, the Board shall credit the member with the largest number of full or partial months of service credit for which full payment has been made, determined in the manner provided in subsection F of § 51.1-142.2 and without regard to the method by which payment was made, valued at the cost determined under subdivision B 4 of § 51.1-142.2; shall recertify the certified subsidy to reflect the service credit so credited, and subdivision D 6 applies to that recertification only so far as it provides for the reservation of an increase; and shall refund, to the member's estate and to any employer that paid on the member's behalf in proportion to the amounts each paid, any amount paid that exceeds the cost of the service credit so credited. The Board shall pay a refund required by the preceding sentence within 60 days after it credits the service credit, except that to the extent the refund may not be paid without adverse federal tax consequence the Board shall apply that amount to the purchase of additional service credit for the member at the standard cost. The Board shall credit the service credit required by this subdivision no later than 60 days after it learns of the member's death, and shall state in the report required by subsection J every failure to credit it within that period. A state agency that employs or last employed a member whose election the Board has accepted shall give the Board written notice of the member's death no later than 30 days after the agency learns of it, and the Board shall state every failure of a state agency to give that notice of which the Board becomes aware in the report required by subsection J. Notwithstanding subdivision C 1 of § 51.1-165 and so much of subsection F of § 51.1-165.01 as requires benefits to be paid in accordance with § 51.1-163, where clause (i) of this subdivision applies and the member dies after the Board has received the notification of the member's effective date of retirement required by subdivision D 1 and before that date, the Board shall determine the benefit payable under this subdivision under § 51.1-162 as on the death of a member in service, computed on the member's creditable service, including the service credit credited under this subdivision; and for the purposes of § 51.1-162 the requirement that the member be in service shall not apply. Nothing in the preceding sentence reduces a benefit payable under subdivision C 2 of § 51.1-165, and where that subdivision applies the Board shall pay the benefit it provides or the benefit determined under the preceding sentence, whichever is greater. Where the member dies, the Board shall give written notice of the service credit credited under this subdivision, of the benefit payable to the member's survivor or beneficiary and of the computation of it, of any right under subsection C of § 51.1-162 of a person entitled to any benefit payable on the member's death to waive the allowance and of the period stated in that subsection within which the waiver must be made, of any right of such a person under subsection B of § 51.1-162 to elect to be paid the member's accumulated contributions or a lump sum in lieu of any other benefit under that section, of the amount of any refund required by this subdivision, and of the reasons for the Board's determinations, to the member's estate, to each person entitled to any benefit payable on the member's death or, where that person is a minor or is under a disability, to that person's guardian, conservator, committee, or other fiduciary and, where there is none, to the person to whom the Board pays or would pay the benefit, to any person who claimed any benefit payable on the member's death and whom the Board determined is not entitled to it, and to any employer that paid on the member's behalf, no later than 30 days after the earlier of the date on which it credits the service credit and the last day on which this subdivision requires it to be credited or, as to a person whose entitlement or non-entitlement to any benefit payable on the member's death, or the amount of any benefit payable to that person, the Board determines after the date determined under the preceding clause, no later than 30 days after that determination; a person to whom that notice is required to be given may seek review of any determination stated in it under subsection M; and the period within which such a person may seek review under subsection M of a determination stated in that notice runs from the date the notice is given to that person or, where this subdivision requires the notice to be given to another person in respect of that person, from the date it is given to that other person. The Board shall state in the report required by subsection J every failure to give the notice required by the preceding sentence to a person within the period this subdivision allows for the giving of it to that person. Where no retirement allowance is payable under § 51.1-162 on the member's death, whether because the person entitled to any benefit payable on the member's death elects under § 51.1-162 to be paid the member's accumulated contributions or a lump sum in lieu of any other benefit under that section or because no such allowance is payable to any person, the Board shall release the amount settled under this subdivision and shall restore it to the amount available under the limitation stated in the first sentence of subdivision D 2 only to the extent the amount settled has not been paid to the retirement system under subsection N, no later than 60 days after the election or, where no such allowance is payable to any person, no later than 60 days after the Board determines that no such allowance is payable, notwithstanding the requirement of subdivision D 2 that an amount settled remain charged against that limitation. Where an amount released under the preceding sentence would not have been released had a determination of the Board been as it stands after reconsideration under subsection M or after judicial review, the Board shall reinstate the amount released, together with any contingency amount released with it, no later than 30 days after the determination is reconsidered or reviewed, and the rule of this subdivision that no certified subsidy is settled more than once does not prevent the settlement of an amount so reinstated. Subsection E does not prevent the crediting of service credit required by this subdivision. Where the member is approved for a disability retirement allowance, the Board shall recertify the certified subsidy to reflect the commencement of a disability retirement allowance, and subdivision D 6 applies to that recertification only so far as it provides for the reservation of an increase. The certified subsidy for an election to which clause (i), (ii), or (iii) of this subdivision applies and that is not settled under subdivision 1 is settled, and subsection N applies to that settlement as it applies to a settlement under subdivision 1, on the earliest of (a) the date of the member's death, (b) the effective date of a disability retirement allowance approved for the member, and (c) the effective date of a retirement that the member is unable to take as a qualifying retirement by reason of an error of the Board. The Board shall make every recertification required by this subdivision before making the redetermination required by this subdivision, except that where a recertification becomes required after the redetermination has been made the Board shall make the recertification and shall thereupon redetermine the certified subsidy again as this subdivision requires. The Board shall, no later than 90 days after that date or, where that date is the date of the member's death, no later than 90 days after the date on which the Board learns of the death, redetermine the certified subsidy in the manner provided in subdivision 1 but substituting that date for the member's actual effective date of retirement, the amount settled shall be the amount so redetermined or the amount last certified and reserved for the election, whichever is less, and the Board shall release the amount by which the amount reserved for the election, together with the contingency amount reserved under subdivision D 3, exceeds the amount settled. Where clause (iii) of this subdivision applies and, by the member’s final qualifying retirement date or, where the member has none, by the completion date stated in the certification, the member has neither died nor been approved for a disability retirement allowance nor retired, the certified subsidy for the election is settled on that date, and this subdivision and subsection N apply to that settlement as they apply to a settlement on a date stated in clause (a), (b), or (c) of this subdivision, except that the period within which the redetermination required by this subdivision must be made runs from the date on which the Board determines that clause (iii) of this subdivision applies or, where the Board does not make that determination within the period allowed to it by this subdivision, from the expiration of that period. The Board shall determine whether clause (iii) of this subdivision applies to an election no later than 60 days after the member's final qualifying retirement date or, where the member has none, after the completion date stated in the certification; shall give the member written notice of the determination and of the reasons for it no later than 15 days after making it; and shall state in the report required by subsection J every failure to make that determination, or to give that notice, within the period this subdivision allows for it. A determination under the preceding sentence is a determination of the Board for the purposes of subsection M. No certified subsidy is settled under this subdivision after the reservation for the election has been released in accordance with subdivision D 7 or after a determination under subdivision 2 or 3 has taken effect, and no certified subsidy is settled more than once under this subdivision or under subdivision 1. The Board shall not make a determination under subdivision 2, and shall not give the notice required by subdivision 2, while an application of the member for a disability retirement allowance under this chapter is pending, and a determination made in contravention of this sentence is of no effect. No period stated in subdivision 2 or in this subdivision, whether it is a period within which the Board is required to act or a period within which a determination is required to take effect, runs while such an application is pending or while a request the filing of which tolls the running of the member’s final qualifying retirement date under subsection M is pending, except that the aggregate of all such non-running by reason of such requests shall not exceed 120 days, and a failure of the Board to act upon such a request within the period allowed to it for acting upon the request shall not extend that aggregate. For the purposes of this subdivision an application for a disability retirement allowance is pending from the date on which the member files it until the date on which the Board first acts upon it, and such a request is pending from the date on which the member files it until the date on which the Board first acts upon it. Where a determination under subdivision 2 or 3 has taken effect and the member is thereafter approved for a disability retirement allowance under this chapter upon an application filed no later than the date on which the determination took effect, or upon an application as to which the Board was required by subsection H of § 51.1-156 to waive the ninety-day notification period set forth in subsection A or B of that section, or thereafter dies in a case to which clause (i) of this subdivision applies, or the Board thereafter determines that the member was unable to retire in a qualifying retirement by reason of an error of the Board, the Board shall rescind the determination and shall restore or credit service credit as this subdivision provides, no later than 60 days after the date on which the member is so approved, or dies, or the Board makes that determination, and the certified subsidy for the election is settled under this subdivision notwithstanding that the rescinded determination had taken effect, and notwithstanding that the reservation for the election has been released in accordance with subdivision D 7. The Board shall pay a refund required by the preceding sentence within 60 days after it credits the service credit, except that where the member has died the Board shall pay the whole of the refund to the member's estate and to any employer that paid on the member's behalf in proportion to the amounts each paid and shall apply no amount of it to the purchase of service credit, and except that in every other case, to the extent the refund may not be paid without adverse federal tax consequence, the Board shall apply that amount to the purchase of additional service credit for the member at the standard cost. Where a determination under subdivision 2 was made or took effect before the Board learned of an involuntary separation of the member occurring on or before the member's final qualifying retirement date as it stood immediately before the determination was made or, if later, immediately before the determination took effect, the Board shall rescind the determination and shall restore the service credit reduced under it no later than 60 days after the date on which the Board learns of the separation, this subdivision applies to the election as though the determination had not been made, and the Board shall reinstate the reservation and any contingency amount released on account of the determination, in the manner this subdivision requires in the case of a reservation released otherwise than in accordance with subdivision D 7, no later than the date of the rescission, and the member’s final qualifying retirement date is the first day of the second month following the month in which the Board rescinds the determination. A member who retires in a retirement that is not a qualifying retirement has not retired in a qualifying retirement for the purposes of the preceding sentence. Where the first day stated in the preceding sentence has passed when the condition stated in the proviso to that sentence is first satisfied, the member's final qualifying retirement date is, notwithstanding that sentence, the first day of the second month following the month in which that condition is first satisfied, and the member's retirement in a qualifying retirement on or before the date so determined satisfies the requirement of this subdivision that the member retire no later than 90 days after the date of the involuntary separation.
