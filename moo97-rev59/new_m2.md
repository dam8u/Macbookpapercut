A member, retiree, beneficiary, applicant, personal representative of a deceased member's estate, person who claimed any benefit payable on the death of a member and whom the Board determined is not entitled to it, or employer that paid on a member's behalf, adversely affected by a determination of the Board under this section, or by a failure of the Board to make a determination or to give a notice within a period required by this section, or by a failure of the Board to state in a certification or notice a matter this section requires to be stated in it, may obtain, upon written request filed within 30 days after the notice of the determination is given or, in the case of a failure to make a determination or to give a notice, at any time before the Board makes the determination or gives the notice, or, in the case of a failure to state a matter, within the period provided by the provision that requires the matter to be stated, written reconsideration by the Board stating the reasons for its decision. Every determination, certification, redetermination, recertification, reduction, cancellation, rescission, restoration, crediting of service credit, placement on a waiting list, expiration of a reservation, denial of an election, and refusal to accept an election under this section is a determination of the Board for the purposes of this subsection, whether or not another provision of this section expressly provides for review of it. The Board shall act upon a request for reconsideration within 90 days. The filing of a request for reconsideration under this subsection, and the filing of a request under the definition of "final qualifying retirement date" in subsection A, tolls the running of the member's final qualifying retirement date and of any period within which the member or applicant must act under this section or under subdivision B 4 f of § 51.1-142.2, until 30 days after the Board acts upon the request, except that (i) no second or subsequent request for reconsideration of the same determination or of the same failure, and no second or subsequent request under that definition, tolls any period under this section or under subdivision B 4 f of § 51.1-142.2, and (ii) the aggregate of all tolling under this subsection shall not exceed 120 days, excluding any period after the expiration of the 90 days allowed by this subsection and before the Board acts upon the request. An application that is the subject of a request for reconsideration under this subsection retains its filing date for the purposes of subdivisions D 4 and D 5 until the Board acts upon the request, and the Board shall act upon the application no later than 15 days after it acts upon the request. Nothing in this subsection limits the right of a member, retiree, beneficiary, applicant, personal representative of a deceased member's estate, person who claimed any benefit payable on the death of a member and whom the Board determined is not entitled to it, or employer that paid on a member's behalf, aggrieved by a determination of the Board under this section, or by such a failure, to judicial review pursuant to Article 5 (§ 2.2-4025 et seq.) of the Administrative Process Act.</u>
