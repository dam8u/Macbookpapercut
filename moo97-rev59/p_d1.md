<u>**D. 1.** The Board shall not accept an election until the Board has, in writing, certified to the member and reserved the certified subsidy for that election under this subsection. The Board shall determine the certified subsidy for an election no later than 30 days after the member's application is completed. The Board's certification shall state the matters required by subdivision B 4 c of § 51.1-142.2, the filing date of the member's complete application, the amount of service credit to be purchased, the certified subsidy, the valuation date, the completion date by which the member must complete payment, the member's anticipated earliest qualifying retirement date and anticipated final qualifying retirement date, the member's unreduced retirement eligibility date determined with the service credit to be purchased as certified, the member's right to cancel the election under subdivision G 5 at any time before completing payment, and the member's obligations under subsections E, F, and G. The Board shall not accept an election until the member has filed with the Board a written acknowledgment of the member's obligations under subsections E, F, and G. The Board's certification shall also state that, where a member whose election the Board has accepted dies after the Board has received the notification of the member's effective date of retirement required by this subdivision and before that date, the benefit payable on the member's death is determined under subdivision G 4 and under § 51.1-162 as on the death of a member in service and not under subdivision C 1 of § 51.1-165 or under subsection F of § 51.1-165.01; that subdivision C 2 of § 51.1-165, where it applies, entitles the person named as contingent annuitant to the benefit that subdivision provides or to the benefit so determined, whichever is greater; that no other option elected under § 51.1-165 or § 51.1-165.01 governs that benefit; that subsection B of § 51.1-162 provides a retirement allowance on the death of a member in service only where the member is survived by a spouse, a minor child or a parent and no benefit is payable under subsection C of that section; that subsection C of § 51.1-162 provides a retirement allowance to a surviving spouse where the member's death results from a cause compensable under the Virginia Workers' Compensation Act (§ 65.2-100 et seq.); and that where no allowance is payable under subsection B or C of § 51.1-162, subsection A of that section provides for the payment of the member's accumulated contributions. A failure to state in the Board's certification every matter required by the preceding sentence is a determination of the Board for the purposes of subsection M, and the Board shall state every such failure in the report required by subsection J. The member shall file the notice of retirement required by § 51.1-153, stating as the effective date of retirement a date on which the member will retire in a qualifying retirement, no later than 30 days after the date on which the Board gives the member the written statement of the member's earliest qualifying retirement date and final qualifying retirement date first required by the definition of "final qualifying retirement date" in subsection A. Nothing in this subdivision requires the member to file that notice any period before the effective date of retirement stated in it. A notice filed within the period allowed by this subdivision satisfies § 51.1-153 notwithstanding that it is filed after the effective date of retirement stated in it; and where such a notice is filed after that effective date, the member's retirement shall take effect on that date and the retirement allowance shall be payable retroactively to that date, subject to subsection E of § 51.1-153 and provided that the effective date stated is on or after the day following the member's last day of service. The Board shall state the period allowed by this subdivision in the written statement required by that definition. Where the Board gives that written statement, any notice of a later approved date required by that definition, or any notice of a date required by that definition upon the Board's action upon a request, later than the time required by that definition, the member's final qualifying retirement date is extended by the number of days by which the statement or notice is late, the member may state an effective date of retirement that is no earlier than the first day of the month following the month in which the Board gives the statement or notice, and the Board shall state every such delay in the report required by subsection J. The Board shall state, in the written statement required by that definition and in every notice of a later approved date required by that definition, (i) that where the Board gives the statement or notice later than the time required by that definition the member's final qualifying retirement date is extended by the number of days by which the statement or notice is late, that the date so extended is subject to the limits stated in that definition, and that where the date so extended is not the first day of a month it is the first day of the next month; (ii) that the member may state as the effective date of retirement a date no earlier than the first day of the month following the month in which the Board gives the statement or notice, and that nothing in this subdivision prevents the member from stating an earlier effective date of retirement to which the member is otherwise entitled, subject to subsection E of § 51.1-153; and (iii) that where the Board mails the statement or notice, every period that runs from the giving of it is extended by three days.</u>
