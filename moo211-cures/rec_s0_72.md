**Five corrections to this record, each a demonstrated error of fact and none of them a change of position.**

1. **R2 and R3 — the title authority does not hold.** §5.47 direction 1 row 7 and §6.0-70's T2 item both cite *Dean v. Paolicelli*, 194 Va. 219 (1952) for the sufficiency of a title line. **That case has no title holding**; it decides eligibility for state office under art. II, § 32 and special legislation under the 1902 Constitution's art. IV, § 64. The authority that does hold is ***City of Richmond v. Pace*, 127 Va. 274, 103 S.E. 647 (1920)** — a title is sufficient if it gives notice of the general subject and of the interests likely to be affected, and unnamed subjects are fine if germane or cognate. **The proposition this record asserted is correct Virginia law; only the case was wrong.** The consequence runs the other way from the one the record implied: under *Pace*, **T2 was never constitutionally required** — the death-benefit provisions were already germane to the stated object — so T2 is a better title and was not a save. *Dean* stays in the file against the eligibility classifications in subsections B and C, where it is good authority.
2. **R1 and R6 — row 3's conforming-amendment column does not hold.** The column says of *"may make or change that designation … at any time before the member's death"* that no conforming amendment is needed because *"the words are tied to A, so the statement follows A if A is amended."* **The citation carries the source of the power; it does not carry the timing.** *"At any time before the member's death"* is neither A's language nor A's content — it is this drafter's inference, as §6.0-70 item 2 says in terms — so if A were amended to fix a moment of irrevocability, D 1 would still compel the old proposition. The *"Verified"* column is sound; the conforming column was not. **S5's saving clause cures it, § 51.1-162 being in S5's list.**
